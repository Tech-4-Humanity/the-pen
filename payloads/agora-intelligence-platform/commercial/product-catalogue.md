# Agora Commercial Product Catalogue

Status: PARTIAL  
Purpose: Turn Agora into reusable T4H commercial offers before runtime deployment.

## Core product line

| Product | Buyer | Primary value | Delivery model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research Media Lab | Universities, institutes | Convert media/discourse into research-ready graphs and evidence packs | Annual licence + services |
| Civic Dialogue Intelligence | Councils, agencies | Understand community narratives and competing viewpoints | SaaS + managed insights |
| Enterprise Narrative Monitor | Corporates, brands | Track reputation, stakeholder narratives, misinformation, and emerging issues | Managed service |
| Education Debate Studio | Schools, universities | Teach critical thinking through evidence-backed multi-perspective debates | Subscription |
| Creator Memory Vault | Creators, educators | Preserve media, transcripts, rights, and searchable community memory | Monthly plan |
| Legal Evidence Archive | Law firms, compliance teams | Convert media/source material into exportable evidence packs | Per matter |
| NDIS Story Repository | Providers, families, practitioners | Capture participant stories, outcomes, evidence, and longitudinal progress | Per participant / provider licence |
| White-label Community Platform | NGOs, associations, franchises | Branded media, debate, memory, and evidence platform | Enterprise |

## Offer packaging

### Research Media Lab

Includes:
- media ingestion
- transcript pipeline
- entity and relationship extraction
- community clustering
- exportable datasets
- research evidence packs
- reproducible methodology notes

Differentiator:
- Turns messy media and comments into reusable research infrastructure.

### Civic Dialogue Intelligence

Includes:
- public meeting media ingestion
- submissions/comments ingestion
- community viewpoint clustering
- debate summaries
- issue tracking
- evidence packs for councillors and staff

Differentiator:
- Converts public discourse into traceable decisions and transparent community understanding.

### Enterprise Narrative Monitor

Includes:
- brand/topic monitoring
- stakeholder narrative mapping
- risk escalation
- executive briefing packs
- source-linked debate summaries

Differentiator:
- Goes beyond sentiment into competing narrative structures and evidence-backed positions.

### Education Debate Studio

Includes:
- topic builder
- persona debates
- source citation enforcement
- teacher review mode
- classroom exports

Differentiator:
- Teaches disagreement, evidence, and reasoning rather than generic chatbot answers.

### Creator Memory Vault

Includes:
- creator media library
- automatic transcripts
- searchable archive
- rights/attribution metadata
- shareable evidence pages

Differentiator:
- Turns creator output into a durable, searchable, ownable memory asset.

### Legal Evidence Archive

Includes:
- source preservation
- transcript creation
- checksum receipts
- artefact manifests
- export packs

Differentiator:
- Makes media-derived evidence easier to inspect, preserve, and reuse.

### NDIS Story Repository

Includes:
- story capture
- goals/outcomes mapping
- family/practitioner notes
- longitudinal evidence timeline
- export packs for reviews

Differentiator:
- Converts human stories into respectful, traceable evidence without losing context.
