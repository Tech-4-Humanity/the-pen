Interlock contract

Child
Gives attempts, questions, reactions and preferences. Receives support, scaffolds, feedback and belonging.
Parent
Gives context, home support, consent and observations. Receives clarity, guidance and progress evidence.
Teacher
Gives instruction, observation and adaptation. Receives reduced admin, better signals and family clarity.
School
Gives policy, safety, resources and escalation. Receives cohort insight, risk visibility and outcome evidence.
Provider
Gives specialist support and evidence. Receives continuity, context and goals.
Platform
Gives orchestration, memory, suggestions and receipts. Must not become a hidden decision-maker.
