## HITL rule

No human checkpoint is required for discovery, classification, binding, generation, verification, dedupe, reconciliation, document generation, register updates or issue updates.

Human approval is required only for:

- production deployment
- public promotion
- destructive deletion
- legal/financial commitment
- irreversible credential/security action

## Evidence states

- REAL: live source, receipt, runtime proof or linked evidence exists
- PARTIAL: source exists but binding/proof is incomplete
- SPEC: structure is designed but not implemented
- PRETEND: rejected as non-evidence
