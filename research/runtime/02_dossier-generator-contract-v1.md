# Dossier Generator Contract v1.0

Input:
- theme_id

Output:
- Executive Reality
- Customer Problem and Outcome
- What We Know
- What We Need To Prove
- Hypothesis and Uncertainty
- Activities and Experiments
- Evidence Chain
- Asset Factory
- Commercialisation
- Audit, R&D Tax and Governance
- Reuse and Propagation
- Decision Record

Rule:
The dossier is generated from the object graph and never becomes the source of truth.

Source of truth:
Theme -> Question -> Uncertainty -> Hypothesis -> Activity -> Experiment -> Evidence -> Asset -> Product -> Revenue -> Claim -> Reuse
