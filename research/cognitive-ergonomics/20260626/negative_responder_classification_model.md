# Negative Responder Classification Model

## Response classes

- Positive responder
- Neutral responder
- Negative responder
- Conditional responder
- High-dependency responder

## Signals

### Performance
- Lower completion rates
- Increased errors
- Higher rework

### Cognitive
- Increased overwhelm
- Reduced confidence
- Reduced persistence

### Behavioural
- Avoidance
- Over-reliance on AI
- Verification failure

## Risk score

Risk Index =
(Overload + Dependency + Error Acceptance + Confidence Loss) / 4

Thresholds:
- 0.0–1.5 Low
- 1.5–3.0 Moderate
- 3.0–5.0 High

High-risk cohorts require additional safeguards and human support pathways.
