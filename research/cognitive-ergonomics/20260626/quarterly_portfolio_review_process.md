# Quarterly Portfolio Review Process

1. Re-score every active idea.
2. Kill bottom 10% by value/effort ratio.
3. Promote a maximum of five initiatives to execution.
4. Confirm owners, budgets, evidence paths, and outputs.
5. Review commercial conversion metrics.
6. Review grants, partners, and publications.
7. Publish a quarterly portfolio report.

Success metrics:
- Papers produced
- Revenue generated
- External partners onboarded
- Runtime receipts issued
- Studies moved from PARTIAL to REAL.
