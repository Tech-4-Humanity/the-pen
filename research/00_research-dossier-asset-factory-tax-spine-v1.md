# Research Dossier Asset Factory Tax Spine v1.0

Status: PARTIAL until bridge/runtime ledger binding.

The Asset Factory must generate tax and audit evidence as a by-product of research work.

Core chain:

Hypothesis -> Uncertainty -> Activity -> Experiment -> Result -> Asset -> Commercial Path -> Audit Evidence

Every asset must carry its origin story:

- linked dossier
- linked research question
- hypothesis
- uncertainty
- activity performed
- experiment method
- inputs used
- outputs created
- evidence objects
- failed attempts
- result
- learning
- reuse path
- commercial path
- tax relevance
- audit grade
- cost trace
- owner
- date range
- receipt

Asset classes:

1. Research assets: hypothesis cards, experiment logs, benchmark results, failed attempt notes, literature reviews, test protocols.
2. Evidence assets: commits, screenshots, logs, prompts, outputs, emails, invoices, cloud usage, receipts.
3. Commercial assets: sales decks, pricing sheets, landing pages, ROI calculators, partner packs, proposals.
4. Governance assets: consent records, privacy assessments, IP notes, risk registers, ethics notes, R&D claim papers.

R&D strength:

- High: directly tied to uncertainty, experiment, technical learning, or failed attempts.
- Medium: supports research context or the evidence chain.
- Low: commercial or content derivative.
- Not R&D: useful but not claim-supporting.

Operating rule:

A commercial asset can come from R&D, but it is not automatically R&D.

The tax trail is:

Asset -> Hypothesis -> Uncertainty -> Experiment -> Evidence -> Cost -> Audit

The business trail is:

Asset -> Offer -> Customer -> Revenue -> Reuse

No origin story, no claim strength.
No uncertainty, no R&D strength.
No evidence, no audit strength.
No reuse path, no factory value.
