# Research Dossier Operating Model v1.0

Status: REAL once commit receipt and fetch verification are present.

Purpose: turn research into an operating asset factory, not a note library.

Core model:
Theme -> Question -> Uncertainty -> Hypothesis -> Activity -> Experiment -> Evidence -> Asset -> Product -> Revenue -> Claim -> Reuse

The dossier is a view over this object graph.

Canonical dossier pages:
1. Executive Reality
2. Customer Problem and Outcome
3. What We Know
4. What We Need To Prove
5. Hypothesis and Uncertainty
6. Activities and Experiments
7. Evidence Chain
8. Asset Factory
9. Commercialisation
10. Audit, R&D Tax and Governance
11. Reuse and Propagation
12. Decision Record

Business decisions each dossier must support:
- Fund
- Stop
- Publish
- Protect
- Sell
- Claim
- Reuse
- Archive

Customer test:
If the customer problem, consequence, proof, offer, and trust boundary cannot be explained clearly, the dossier is not ready for commercialisation.

Tax and audit test:
If uncertainty, hypothesis, activity, experiment, evidence, failures, date range, owner, and cost trace are missing, the dossier is weak for R&D/tax purposes.

Asset Factory rule:
Every asset must carry its origin story. No origin story, no claim strength. No uncertainty, no R&D strength. No evidence, no audit strength. No reuse path, no factory value.

Evidence grades:
A = source-generated and contemporaneous.
B = system-generated.
C = reconstructed.
D = memory-based.

Rule: D can explain A, but D cannot replace A.

Minimum 1.0 acceptance:
- all 12 sections completed or explicitly marked blocked
- at least one customer journey
- at least one hypothesis
- at least one uncertainty statement
- at least one activity or experiment record
- evidence placeholders or evidence objects for major claims
- commercial pathway defined
- audit and R&D fields present
- reuse mapping present
- decision state selected
- owner assigned
- next action executable
- receipt attached

Reality state:
REAL requires executed evidence and receipt.
PARTIAL means architecturally sound but not fully evidenced.
BLOCKED means dependency, credential, legal, destructive, or external authority boundary prevents completion.
