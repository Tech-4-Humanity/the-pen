## Category Claim

The market is not short of apps. It is short of a **coordination runtime**.

Existing tools store fragments:
- [CRM](https://www.salesforce.com/) stores relationships
- [ERP](https://www.sap.com/) stores transactions
- [PM tools](https://asana.com/) store tasks
- [Slack](https://slack.com)/[Teams](https://www.microsoft.com/en/microsoft-teams/) store conversation
- [Docs](https://docs.google.com) store knowledge
- [BI](https://www.tableau.com/) stores indicators

No mainstream system reliably stores and operates: original operational intent · role-native translation · system impact graph · stakeholder readiness · execution confidence · drift detection · evidence binding · reality classification.

**[Chatter](https://ahc.holo-org.com) / [AHC](https://ahc.holo-org.com) owns this layer.**
