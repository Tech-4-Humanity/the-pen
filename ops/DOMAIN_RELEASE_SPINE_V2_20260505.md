## Operating correction

A domain is not a business.
A brand is not always a business.
A product is not always a brand.
A demo is not a product.
A research surface is not automatically a commercial offer.

Every asset must be classified as one of:

- BUSINESS
- BRAND
- PRODUCT
- DEMO_PROOF
- RESEARCH
- INTERNAL_SYSTEM
- SOURCE_ASSET
- HOLD
- MERGE
- STRIP_REUSE
