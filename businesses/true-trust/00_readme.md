## Executive summary

True Trust is a business-in-development built from the idea that debt stress and employment stress should not be treated as separate problems.

The operating thesis:

> Help people keep their home and rebuild stability by restoring income, not merely restructuring debt.

Banks, employers, recruiters, families, schools, insurers, government programs and communities all carry cost when a recoverable person falls through the cracks. True Trust becomes the trusted intervention layer that detects financial pressure, captures consent, verifies recoverability, routes the person to work, and reports evidence-backed outcomes.

This is not a debt-advice business by default. It is an income-restoration, trust-verification, employment-pathway, partner-routing and outcome-evidence business.

## Why now

Public market signals support the timing:

- Roy Morgan reported 26.8% of Australian mortgage holders at risk of mortgage stress in the three months to March 2026, equivalent to about 1.447 million people.
- ABS reported 337,900 job vacancies in February 2026, up 2.7% from November 2025.
- HILDA, SEIFA, Labour Market Insights and Jobs and Skills Australia can support vulnerability, recoverability, household pressure, and labour-demand modelling.

The signal is mismatch: people under financial pressure exist at the same time as employers still have demand.

## Core insight

Most systems split the person into fragments:

- bank sees arrears
- recruiter sees job search
- employer sees vacancy
- family sees stress
- government sees welfare or compliance load
- court/legal services see downstream failure
- community sees participation loss

True Trust treats the person as a recoverable value asset with consent, evidence, trust, and partner outcomes.

## Positioning

**Primary line:** True Trust restores income before financial pressure becomes permanent damage.

**Bank line:** Reduce default risk by funding recoverability, not just hardship processing.

**Employer line:** Find verified, motivated workers with a recovery pathway and evidence trail.

**Community line:** Keep people housed, working, paying, parenting, participating and stable.

True Trust is:

True Trust is not, unless separately licensed and reviewed:

- a credit provider
- a debt collector
- a financial adviser
- a mortgage broker
- a debt negotiator
- a hardship decision-maker
- a coercive employment pathway
