## Product Touchpoints

### Outcome Ready
RAM turns fragmented readiness, NDIS, practitioner, provider, audit, claim, and evidence assets into validated packs.

### Reading Buddy
RAM validates content, intervention, family, teacher, signal, and reporting assets.

### WorkFamilyAI
RAM validates and packages role maps, 729/1000-agent assets, workflows, and reusable organisational capability objects.

### SchoolFamilyAI
RAM structures school, parent, teacher, student, worksheet, curriculum, and family role assets.

### CommunityFamilyAI
RAM converts community programs, stakeholders, evidence, and partner materials into reusable social infrastructure assets.

### Augmented Humanity Coach
RAM becomes an AHC delivery capability: RAM Clean, RAM Validate, RAM Portfolio, RAM Operating Memory, RAM Governance Evidence Pack.

### GC-BAT
RAM supplies evidence-bound governance, board, audit, risk, control, and foresight assets.

### AI4Tradies
RAM cleans quote templates, job photos, proof assets, case studies, service pages, and lead magnets.

### AI4Professionals
RAM turns consultant/professional expertise into reusable IP, portfolio, proposal, and capability packs.

### Augmented Memories
RAM structures personal, family, legacy, digital memory, consent, provenance, and continuity assets.

### Digital Executor / Digital Continuity
RAM inventories non-crypto digital assets, continuity instructions, heirs/owners, evidence gaps, and controlled reuse paths.

### Drug Resilience Atlas
RAM validates research sources, calculator versions, survey assets, debate topics, and evidence cards.

### AI Sweet Spots
RAM turns research artifacts into validated cohort, product, marketing, and evidence assets.

### Agent Channel / Knowledge Drop / Agent Orchestrator
RAM extracts widgets, prompts, schemas, snippets, agent actions, and reusable runtime components from agent work.

### Signal / Places
RAM supplies trusted asset context, evidence, and escalation surfaces across home, car, office, browser, TV, mobile, and other places.

### Synal / Doolittles
RAM gives machine-to-machine and human-to-machine translation persistent memory, lineage, and evidence.

### Portfolio Registry / Business Catalogue
RAM maps assets to businesses, products, offers, stages, evidence state, and reusable capability areas.

### Pricing / Monetisation System
RAM maps reusable assets to SKUs, bundles, margins, pricing, and sellable IP.

### News-to-Business Engine
RAM stores article-derived business opportunities, source evidence, trend assets, product mappings, and duplicate-prevention metadata.

### Thread Recovery Engine
RAM converts old conversations, unfinished tasks, repeated ideas, failed handoffs, and hidden instructions into validated executable assets.

### Bookmarks Execution Engine
RAM converts saved pages and duplicate bookmark occurrences into executable product, research, and opportunity assets.
