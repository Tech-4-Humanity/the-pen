# 82_ram_segment-strategy.md

## Sequence (strict)
1. Internal dogfood (T4H operating system)
2. Founder portfolio (Troy)
3. SMB asset cleanup
4. Enterprise / government knowledge recovery
5. AI governance add-on (RAM as evidence layer)

Each segment unlocks only after the prior is REAL.

## Segment 1 — Internal T4H Operating System (priority)
- Customers: every T4H product internally
- Pain: pen package chaos, bridge orphans, untraceable receipts, unverifiable claims, paper-city assets
- Offer: free internal use, mandatory across all products
- Success metric: every prod promotion in T4H has a paired RAM evidence trail
- Completion gate: dogfood REAL across the touchpoint map

## Segment 2 — Founder Portfolio (Troy)
- Customer: Troy (singular)
- Pain: placeholder fields in CV, no JD tailoring, scattered evidence, no executive proof packs
- Offer: founder-portfolio generation from validated assets
- Success metric: CV + executive + advisory + speaking + standards portfolios generated from REAL evidence only
- Completion gate: Tier-1 founder portfolio published with reality_ledger backing

## Segment 3 — SMB asset cleanup
- Customer profile: consultants, tradies, allied health, NDIS providers, small agencies
- Pain: 10+ years of asset chaos, no reusable IP, no portfolio surface
- Offer: Tier 1-4 (Clean, Lift, Portfolio, Operating Memory)
- Pricing: flat tiers ($499, $1500, $3500, $7500)
- Success metric: 10 paying customers with REAL portfolio cards
- Completion gate: Segment 2 REAL + at least one external pilot at no charge

## Segment 4 — Enterprise / Government knowledge recovery
- Customer profile: departments, councils, health, education, compliance orgs
- Pain: stale policies, untrusted docs, lost evidence, broken handover, knowledge trapped in people
- Offer: bespoke engagements; institutional memory recovery; governance binding
- Pricing: POA
- Success metric: one signed engagement with reality_ledger-backed deliverables
- Completion gate: Segment 3 REAL + ConsentX + GC-BAT live for the customer

## Segment 5 — AI governance add-on
- Customer profile: enterprises adopting AI-generated content at scale
- Pain: hallucination, no provenance, untrusted AI outputs, no evidence chains
- Offer: RAM as the validation + evidence layer for AI outputs
- Attach to: GC-BAT, ConsentX, MyNeuralSignal, WorkFamilyAI, HoloOrg
- Success metric: at least one enterprise binds RAM as their AI evidence layer
- Completion gate: Segments 1-4 REAL

## Cross-segment principles
- RAM never publishes pricing or external content without HITL.
- RAM never claims REAL on customer-facing material without typed evidence.
- RAM respects ConsentX provenance on every external use.
- RAM publishes negative results too: failed validations, broken links, missing evidence. The system is honest by design.

## Sequencing rationale
Internal-first prevents the most common failure mode: selling tooling that doesn't work on its author's own mess. Dogfood completion is the credibility prerequisite for every downstream segment.
