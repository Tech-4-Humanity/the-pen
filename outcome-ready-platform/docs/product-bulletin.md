# Product Bulletin — Outcome Ready

## Summary
Outcome Ready consolidates reading, maths, and wellbeing into a unified platform with shared assessment, intervention, and reporting capabilities.

## Products

### Reading Mate
Literacy, comprehension, and guided reading support.

### Maths Mate
Numeracy, problem solving, and decision-based maths.

### Thrive Mate
Wellbeing, focus, and behavioural support.

### Adult Capability Mode
Application of the same system to adult learning, workplace capability, and productivity.

- AI Sweet Spots Assessments: https://aisweetspots.com/assessments

- CalmBound: https://calmbound-system.vercel.app/

- School Starter Pack
- Provider Starter Pack
- Adult Capability Pack

## Status

Platform is in build phase. Core architecture is defined. Frontend exists in parts. Backend, email, and automation layers are not yet proven in production.
