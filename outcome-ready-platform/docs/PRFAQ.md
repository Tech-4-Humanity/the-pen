# PRFAQ — Outcome Ready Platform

## Press Release

Sydney, Australia — Outcome Ready today announced the launch of its integrated Mate platform, bringing together learning, wellbeing, and human capability into a single system for children, adults, schools, and organisations.

The platform introduces three core modes:

- Reading Mate: literacy, comprehension, and reading confidence
- Maths Mate: numeracy, real-world problem solving, and decision maths
- Thrive Mate: wellbeing, focus, and behavioural support

All modes are powered by a shared assessment and intervention engine that allows users to move from intake to recommendation to measurable improvement.

The platform integrates diagnostic assessments, wellbeing systems, and external signals to support parents, educators, providers, and individuals in improving outcomes in a measurable way.

Outcome Ready is available via outcome-ready.com, with assessment entry points and product packs for families, schools, providers, and enterprises.

## FAQ

### What is Outcome Ready?
Outcome Ready is a platform that combines learning, wellbeing, and capability into a single system with measurable outcomes.

### How is it different from a reading or maths app?
It connects assessment, intervention, and reporting rather than just delivering content.

### Who is it for?
Children, parents, schools, providers, and adults seeking capability improvement.

### What products are included?
Reading Mate, Maths Mate, Thrive Mate, and Adult Capability Mode.

### How are assessments used?
Assessments identify gaps, generate recommendations, and trigger actions.

### How is wellbeing handled?
Through Thrive Mate and integration with systems such as CalmBound and contextual signals.

### Is it safe for children?
The system is designed with consent and child-safe principles, with parent/guardian involvement.

### What outcomes should users expect?
Improved literacy, numeracy, wellbeing, and capability, supported by reports and evidence logs.
