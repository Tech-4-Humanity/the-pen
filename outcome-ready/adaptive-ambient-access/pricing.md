## NDIS Billing Notes

- AAA platform fee is billable under **Assistance with Daily Life** (Core Supports)
- Support Coordination billing: **07_002_0106_8** 
- Registered providers can on-bill platform costs as **non-face-to-face** support delivery
- Invoicing via Outcome Ready Stripe integration (automatic NDIS-format receipts)
