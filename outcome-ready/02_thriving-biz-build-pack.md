# Thriving Biz Build Pack

Status: PARTIAL until pages, forms, automations, telemetry, and sales proof are live.

## Purpose
Thriving Biz is the May-July market-facing brand for providers, allied-health businesses, accountants, advisors, and compliance-heavy service operators facing reform pressure.

It is not only compliance. Compliance is the entry wedge. The larger offer is agentic AI business operations.

## Core message
Stay compliant. Stay operational. Stay paid.

## Buyer segments

| Segment | Pain | Offer | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Provider owner | registration, audits, claims, margin pressure | Provider Readiness Score + Survival Pack | Get your readiness score |
| Practitioner / staff | admin, notes, follow-up, reporting | AI Office + Notes Agent | Reduce admin load |
| Provider entity | documentation, service proof, claim defensibility | Evidence Engine + Audit Defence | Build your evidence layer |
| Accountant / advisor | clients exposed to compliance and ATO/governance gaps | Business Readiness Pack | Run a client readiness scan |
| Growth operator | intake leakage and missed calls | AI Calls + Intake Agent | Capture every enquiry |

## Offer ladder

1. Free/low-cost: Thriving Biz Alert
2. Entry: Provider Readiness Score
3. Starter: NDIS Reform Survival Pack
4. Core: Compliance OS
5. Expansion: AI Office + AI Calls
6. Premium: Audit Defence + Evidence Engine
7. Subscription: Reform Intelligence + monthly evidence reports

## Products

### Thriving Biz Alert
Recurring policy/reform alert feed translated into business actions.

Includes:
- weekly reform summary
- provider action checklist
- deadline alerts
- risk notices
- service-category impact notes

### Provider Readiness Score
Lead-generation diagnostic.

Inputs:
- provider type
- registration status
- service categories
- documentation maturity
- evidence maturity
- worker governance
- claims process
- incident process
- audit history

Outputs:
- readiness score
- risk heatmap
- top 5 gaps
- 30-day action plan
- recommended product pathway

### NDIS Reform Survival Pack
Fast implementation pack for May-July.

Includes:
- registration checklist
- policy gap checklist
- evidence baseline
- audit readiness checklist
- claims documentation checklist
- reform action roadmap
- AI office starter workflows

### Compliance OS
Core operating system.

Modules:
- obligation register
- evidence register
- worker compliance register
- incident workflow
- audit workflow
- corrective action tracker
- policy version tracker
- risk scoring

### AI Office
Reusable agentic business operations pack.

Includes:
- task routing
- follow-up generation
- document drafting
- meeting summaries
- client reminders
- reporting agent
- admin queue

### AI Calls / Intake
Front-door operations.

Includes:
- enquiry capture
- triage
- booking support
- FAQ handling
- escalation rules
- missed-call recovery
- CRM lead creation

### Audit Defence Pack
Evidence and remediation layer.

Includes:
- evidence timeline
- claim-linked documentation
- gap report
- corrective action plan
- audit export pack
- board/owner summary
