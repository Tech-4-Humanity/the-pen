## External positioning

Service Runtime Intelligence helps organisations diagnose the health of the promises they make through services, workflows and operating systems.

It does not replace ServiceNow, Jira, Workday, Salesforce, Celonis or observability tools.

It reads across them to answer a harder question:

> Are our organisational promises healthy, evidenced, owned and economically rational?

## Core thesis

Businesses have spent decades managing systems.

The next phase is managing organisational metabolism:
- how work flows
- where signals are sensed or ignored
- where governance protects or attacks the organisation
- where memory is retained or lost
- where dependencies create silent organ failure
- where services remain alive after they are economically dead

## Differentiation

| Existing category | Common claim | Our differentiated claim |

| ITSM / ServiceNow | Manage requests and workflows | Diagnose whether service promises are healthy |
| Process mining / Celonis | Reveal process execution truth | Add ownership, human friction, evidence, service debt and intervention logic |
| Observability | Monitor software health | Monitor organisational promise health |
| Employee experience | Capture sentiment and survey data | Bind human signal to service, workflow, owner, cost, risk and action |

| AI-agent platforms | Automate tasks | Give agents a metabolism map before they act |

## First wedge

Service Catalog Reality Audit.

It is narrow enough to sell, broad enough to prove the category, and directly connected to existing customer systems.

## Category warning

Do not sell this first as a full organisational metabolism platform. That is too broad.

Sell the first proof:

> Give us your service catalog and request history. We will show where your organisation is getting sick.
