# Disease Taxonomy v1

| Disease | Business condition | Signals | Intervention | Economic impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Organisational Cholesterol | Work cannot flow through handoffs | slow fulfilment, wait states, queue buildup | remove handoff, automate routing, clarify fulfiller | delay cost and productivity loss |
| Knowledge Dementia | Knowledge exists but cannot be recalled | missing KB, repeat incidents, single-person dependency | extract memory, update KB, assign knowledge owner | rework and key-person risk |
| Autoimmune Governance | Controls attack healthy work | over-approval, shadow IT, bypasses | redesign approval tier and risk rules | productivity drag |
| Silent Organ Failure | Critical hidden dependency can fail | one owner, one system, one spreadsheet, missing backup | redundancy, dependency register, recovery path | outage and continuity risk |
| Nervous System Numbness | Organisation cannot feel pain early | late escalation, low sentiment, no telemetry | add signal capture and intervention trigger | crisis cost and trust loss |
| Chronic Inflammation | Constant low-grade operational stress | high reopen rate, recurring escalations | root-cause review and workflow correction | burnout and support cost |
| Metabolic Syndrome | High volume, low value, high friction | many requests, high cost, low satisfaction | simplify, automate, retire or redesign | margin erosion |
| Approval Hypertension | Decisions are chronically slow | high approval steps, p95 approval delay | lower-risk tiering, delegated approval | opportunity cost |
| Toxic Accumulation | Old services remain alive after value is gone | deprecated items still used, missing replacement | controlled retirement and replacement path | platform clutter and risk |
| Diabetes of Data | Signal exists but action fails | reports everywhere, no interventions | bind signals to owners and actions | insight waste |
