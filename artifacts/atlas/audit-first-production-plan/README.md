# T4H Canonical Research Narrative Standard v1.0 — Audit Edition

## Purpose

This package establishes the audit-first production standard for the Tech4Humanity Research Atlas.

The immediate priority is to produce an audit-ready, evidence-ready and RDTI-ready research corpus. Publishing, recurring characters, Holo-Org journeys, Neural Ennead profiles and broader narrative reuse remain valid secondary uses, but they must not distract from the current audit objective.

## Priority order

1. **ATO / RDTI / scientific audit** — demonstrate the problem, scientific uncertainty, novel contribution, investigation, evidence, limitations and future work.
2. **Public understanding** — use evidence-informed human context to explain why the research matters.
3. **Publishing and narrative expansion** — defer richer longitudinal storytelling until after the audit-ready corpus is complete.

## Production rule

Research comes first. Narrative supports understanding and must never be presented as evidence.

Each subtopic page should make clear:

- why the problem exists;
- what remains scientifically uncertain;
- what is genuinely novel;
- what was or will be investigated;
- what evidence supports the work;
- what limitations and contradictions remain;
- what future research is required;
- why the work matters to people, organisations or society.
