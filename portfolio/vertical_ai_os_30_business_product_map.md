## Executive Position

This is not 30 separate businesses. It is one reusable Vertical AI Operating System with multiple market skins, trust brands, and product wrappers.

Core engine:
- Intake / Front Desk
- Analyse / Diagnose
- Write / Generate
- Book / Schedule
- Quote / Propose
- Convert / Follow-up
- Deliver / Execute
- Evidence / Govern
- Track / Dashboard
- Optimise / Improve
- Retain / Renew
- Loop / Learn

Reusable commercial products:
- AI Front Desk
- AI Quote / Proposal Desk
- AI Booking Desk
- AI Growth Desk
- AI Evidence Desk
- AI Ops Dashboard

- Full Vertical AI OS

## Portfolio Rule

Every business gets:
1. A customer-facing wrapper.
2. A core product bundle.
3. A pricing ladder.
4. A diagnostic route.
5. A dashboard.
6. A proof/evidence layer.
7. A clone path to the next related vertical.

Do not build 30 separate stacks. Build one stack with 30 wrappers.
