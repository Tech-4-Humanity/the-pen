# Operational Intelligence Infrastructure

Status: BRIDGE_READY

## Category
AI-native operational infrastructure for human organisations.

This is not an AI tools bundle. It is the reusable operating layer underneath the portfolio wrappers.

## Buyer demand
Enterprises, regulated sectors, and professional firms do not primarily want chatbots. They want:

- operational intelligence
- auditability
- orchestration
- governance
- evidence
- workforce augmentation
- institutional memory

## Core platform promise
Connect fragmented work, people, systems, documents, decisions, evidence, and actions into one governed operational runtime.
