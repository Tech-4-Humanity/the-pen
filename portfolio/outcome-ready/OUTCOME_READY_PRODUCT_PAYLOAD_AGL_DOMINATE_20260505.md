## 1. Executive Owner Decision

Outcome Ready is not to be treated as a loose platform or vague product ecosystem. It is a commercial outcome engine that turns human activity, supports, learning, intervention, and progress into provable outcomes that unlock funding, trust, reporting, and better decisions.

The product line must be compressed into clear buyer-facing offers while preserving the deeper modular engine behind the scenes.

Priority order:

1. Outcome Ready Platform — subscription core
2. Plan Review Pack Factory — transactional high-margin product
3. Claim-Ready Documentation Pack Generator — claim protection and speed product
4. Provider Evidence Lift — premium cohort/complex-needs product
5. Reading Buddy — flagship intervention brand/product inside Outcome Ready
6. Thriving Kids / Thriving Biz sector packs — segment wrappers using the same outcome engine

Owner rule: no new platform build unless it directly improves revenue, proof, distribution, or customer trust.

## 2. Product Inventory

### 2.1 Core Revenue Products

#### Outcome Ready Platform
Commercial role: foundation subscription product.
Buyer: NDIS providers, allied health providers, disability support organisations, education/support organisations.
Indicative pricing: from AUD 590/month.
Outcome: claims clarity, progress evidence, plan review readiness, reporting visibility.

Core promise:
- Track support activity against goals.
- Convert activity into outcome evidence.
- Reduce documentation chaos.
- Improve review and claim confidence.

#### Plan Review Pack Factory
Commercial role: high-value transactional product.
Buyer: providers and families preparing for plan review.
Indicative pricing: from AUD 1,250 per pack.
Outcome: review-ready evidence pack before deadlines.

Core promise:
- Goal-linked review pack.
- Timeline and support mapping.
- Plain-English participant narrative.
- Outcome tables with consistent metrics.
- Clinician/practitioner checkpoints where required.

#### Claim-Ready Documentation Pack Generator
Commercial role: revenue protection and admin-reduction product.
Buyer: providers managing claim evidence and administrative burden.
Pricing: per pack, subscription inclusion, or bundled with Outcome Ready Platform.
Outcome: better claim documentation before lodgement.

Core promise:
- Assemble claim evidence.
- Structure documents against provider rules.
- Reduce missing evidence.
- Improve first-pass claim confidence.

#### Provider Evidence Lift
Commercial role: premium upsell / consultative product.
Buyer: providers with complex cohorts, neurodiverse participants, psychosocial needs, or hard-to-measure outcomes.
Pricing: by arrangement.
Outcome: stronger baseline and progress measurement for complex supports.

Core promise:
- Structured baseline.
- Progress observation.
- Cognitive profile lenses.
- Goal-linked outcome measurement.
- Evidence chain suitable for serious review conversations.

## 3. Five SKU Commercial Compression

### SKU 1 — Outcome Ready Core
Buyer: Providers.
Price anchor: AUD 590/month.
Promise: Know what is happening, what is missing, and what is ready for claims or review.
Metric: reduction in missing documentation; improved review readiness; claim visibility.

### SKU 2 — Plan Review Pack Factory
Buyer: Providers and families.
Price anchor: AUD 1,250+/pack.
Promise: Turn activity, goals, and progress into review-ready evidence.
Metric: pack completion before review date; practitioner sign-off; fewer missing evidence items.

### SKU 3 — Claim Rescue + Prevention
Buyer: Providers with claim leakage.
Price anchor: AUD 790/month or per-claim pack bundles.
Promise: Find gaps before lodgement and recover failed claims faster.
Metric: rejected claim rate; recovered value; time-to-remediation.

### SKU 4 — Provider Evidence Lift
Buyer: complex providers / cohorts.
Price anchor: by arrangement, AUD 5k–25k+ depending on cohort.
Promise: Create defensible progress evidence for hard-to-measure supports.
Metric: baseline coverage; progress evidence completeness; review confidence.

### SKU 5 — Reading Buddy by Outcome Ready
Buyer: parents, schools, NDIS-related supports, tutors.
Price anchor: consumer subscription + practitioner/school pack.
Promise: Improve reading and prove the improvement.
Metric: reading sessions, comprehension lift, confidence signals, progress reports.

## 4. Sales Motion

### Immediate buyer wedge
Lead with pain:
- missed evidence
- rejected claims
- review scramble
- unclear participant progress
- admin overload

Do not lead with AI, platform, architecture, agents, signal engines, or governance infrastructure.

### Sales path
1. Diagnose leakage or review risk.
2. Sell Claim Rescue / Plan Review Pack.
3. Convert to Outcome Ready Core subscription.
4. Upsell Provider Evidence Lift.
5. Introduce Reading Buddy / Thriving Kids where relevant.

### Owner-level rule
Outcome Ready should sell relief first and sophistication second.
