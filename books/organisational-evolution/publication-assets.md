# Publication Assets Register

## Core messages

- The organisation did not become alive because it used AI.
- A faster hierarchy is still a hierarchy.
- The provider is replaceable. The capability is durable.
- Runtime belongs in the engine room.
- OIKOS is the living organisation.
- Technology is the catalyst. Flourishing is the test.
- Governance must occur during execution, not after the damage is done.
- The ultimate test is whether the organisation leaves the world more capable than it found it.

Use organic and institutional imagery rather than generic robot or circuit-board imagery:

- mycelium and root systems
- living architecture
- rivers and flow
- constellations and capability networks
- rings, memory and accumulated learning
- organisation charts dissolving into adaptive networks
- human figures retained at the centre of authority and meaning
