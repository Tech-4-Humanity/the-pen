# Book Architecture

## Working title

**The Day the Organisation Learned to Live**

### Subtitle

From Hierarchy to Flourishing: a theory, framework and canon of organisational evolution

## Book promise

This book explains why organisations designed for scarce intelligence are changing, how that evolution unfolds, and what a living organisation must become if it is to increase human and ecosystem capability rather than merely automate itself.

## Audience

- Executives and boards
- Organisational designers
- Public-sector leaders
- Researchers and academics
- Technology and AI leaders
- People navigating changing work identities

## Narrative rules

- Each part begins with a human or institutional story.
- Each chapter contains one dominant idea.
- Technical machinery remains subordinate to human and organisational meaning.
- Claims must be marked as observed, theoretical, hypothesised, normative or aspirational where the distinction matters.
- No unsupported numerical multiplier is presented as fact.
- Each chapter ends by creating momentum into the next.

## Definition of done

- Complete, internally consistent manuscript
- Canonical terminology applied throughout
- Evidence placeholders resolved or clearly marked
- Counterarguments included
- Diagrams specified
- Front matter, glossary, references and research agenda complete
- Publication assets derived from the manuscript
