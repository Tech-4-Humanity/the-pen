# Synal — Decoupled Upsell Strategy

## Decision
Synal is valuable, but it must not hold up Doolittles, Command Layer, AHC or the first revenue demos.

Synal should be positioned as an upsell, on-sell, suite layer or later packaging vehicle — not as a dependency for the first commercial proof.

## Core rule
Do not block the first working loop waiting for Synal.

Doolittles can sell and run without the full Synal suite.
Synal can later package, extend and multiply the value.

---

## Product separation

### Doolittles first
Doolittles is the entry product.

It proves:
- one announcement;
- many human translations;
- simple workflow outputs;
- training/docs generated;
- proof panel.

It can be demoed with:
- website;
- email outputs;
- Slack-style mock;
- ServiceNow-style stub;
- LMS stub;
- Salesforce/SAP readiness stubs.

### Command Layer underneath
Command Layer is the engine.

It handles:
- command intake;
- routing;
- YAML;
- action state;
- registry;
- evidence.

### Synal later
Synal becomes the suite surface when the buyer wants:
- browser/app experience;
- widgets;
- mobile;
- HoloWall;
- car/room/surface continuity;
- advanced communication package;
- multi-surface orchestration.

---

## Sales path

### Phase 1 — Doolittles demo
Sell the painkiller:
"One announcement becomes the right message, task, doc, training and proof."

### Phase 2 — Team pilot
Sell to HR/comms/support/PMO:
"We remove translation drag and coordination waste."

### Phase 3 — Enterprise command layer
Sell to CIO/COO:
"We connect decisions to systems with guardrails and proof."

### Phase 4 — Synal upsell
Sell the suite:
"Now put the command layer everywhere — browser, mobile, TV, room, car and widgets."

---

## What not to do
Do not lead with:
- full Synal browser;
- every widget;
- car/TV/home surfaces;
- complete multi-device suite;
- SAP live integration.

That is too much too early and risks slowing the first commercial proof.

## What to lead with
Lead with:
- Doolittles translator;
- Microsoft rollout demo;
- message-to-workflow proof;
- ServiceNow-style ticket;
- training/doc generation;
- proof panel.

---

## Packaging language

### Public simple version
Doolittles is the first product powered by the T4H Command Layer.
Synal is the larger communication suite that can extend it across every device and surface.

### Enterprise version
Start with a command-to-action pilot. Add Synal when you are ready to operationalise the pattern across the organisation.

### Partner version
Doolittles proves the workflow. Synal provides the distribution surface.

---

## Pricing implication

Doolittles Lite and Teams should be priced and sold without requiring Synal.

Synal is:
- add-on;
- enterprise package;
- partner package;
- multi-surface upgrade;
- strategic platform layer.

## Suggested pricing split

| Package | Dependency | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Doolittles Lite | standalone | lead capture / SMB / team demo |
| Doolittles Teams | standalone | first paid pilots |
| Doolittles Enterprise | Command Layer | enterprise proof |
| Synal Suite | upsell | multi-surface operating layer |
| Synal Partner | later | OEM/partner distribution |
