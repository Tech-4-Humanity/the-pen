# Doolittles — Visual + Video Elements Pack

## Purpose
Add Doodly-style explainers, real video, HeyGen avatar moments, and simple UI demo scenes to make Doolittles understandable fast.

## Core visual principle
Start simple. A hand draws the problem. A real person explains the pain. An avatar demonstrates the new command layer. Then the UI proves it.

## First hero video script — 60 seconds

### Title
Doolittles: Talk to the animals

### Scene 1 — Old world
Visual: whiteboard drawing of executive making announcement.
Narration:
"In most organisations, a leader says one thing and every team hears something different."

Visual: email chains, meetings, confused people, tools asleep.
Narration:
"Slack hears noise. SAP hears nothing. ServiceNow gets tickets too late. Training starts after confusion begins."

### Scene 2 — Doolittles enters
Visual: Dr Doolittle / AHC translator stands between leader, people, and tools.
Narration:
"Doolittles translates one announcement into the language every person, tool and system needs."

### Scene 3 — Day-one activation
Visual: announcement becomes YAML, Slack messages, ServiceNow ticket, LMS module, Salesforce impact, SAP readiness stub.
Narration:
"Johnny gets French email. Mary gets Slack bullets. The support desk gets a knowledge base. Training starts. Finance models cost. Systems start preparing on day one."

### Scene 4 — Before/after
Visual: old timeline 6-8 weeks collapses into 24-72 hours.
Narration:
"We do not wait for change meetings. We give every human and every system the signal at the same time."

### Scene 5 — Close
Visual: HoloWall readiness board.
Narration:
"Say it once. Everyone hears it correctly. Everything starts moving."

Show what happens to ServiceNow before and after Doolittles.

- creates late support material.

After:
- receives command object;
- creates change record;
- drafts KB;
- posts Slack update;
- logs proof.
