# SmartPark: Global Parking Asset Operating System

## Round 1 — Bigger: the parking spot is not the business

SmartPark is not a parking app.

It is the entry point into one of the largest unmanaged asset classes on earth: physical vehicle waiting space.

Every city, shopping centre, stadium, hospital, airport, school, office park, warehouse, apartment block, tourist precinct and roadside already has parking inventory. Most of it is poorly mapped, poorly priced, weakly governed, politically sensitive, and commercially underused.

Today, parking is treated as a nuisance, a compliance problem, or a minor revenue line.

That is the wrong frame.

Parking is urban inventory. Parking is logistics inventory. Parking is retail inventory. Parking is transport inventory. Parking is energy inventory. Parking is future autonomous fleet infrastructure.

The big question is not: where can I park?

The real question is: who owns the intelligence layer for where vehicles wait, charge, load, clean, service, queue, stage, drop off, pick up, rest and reposition?

SmartPark begins with painted lines on asphalt, but the ambition is the global operating system for vehicle dwell space.

## Round 2 — Bigger again: driverless vehicles make waiting space strategic

Driverless vehicles do not eliminate parking.

They make parking more important.

A driverless car still needs somewhere to wait. A delivery robot needs somewhere to stage. A rideshare fleet needs somewhere to reposition. An autonomous shuttle needs a managed stop. An EV needs somewhere to charge. A maintenance bot needs somewhere to dock. A council needs to know what spaces exist, what they are worth, who is using them, and whether they are serving local economic goals.

When vehicles no longer need a driver sitting inside them, parking stops being a static end-state and becomes a dynamic orchestration layer.

Spaces will be allocated by purpose, time, vehicle type, service level, energy demand, local policy, mobility need, commercial priority and community value.

That future will need:

- a global registry of spaces
- verified location and ownership data
- rules for usage, timing, access and pricing
- sensors or signal proxies
- payment rails
- local government and private property integration
- retail and precinct optimisation
- EV and autonomous fleet interfaces
- enforcement-to-service workforce transition
- asset monetisation models
- trust, consent, audit and governance

The company that maps and governs the spaces can become the coordination layer for the next transport economy.

The largest parking company in the world may not own car parks. It may own the live asset graph that every vehicle, operator, council, property owner, retailer and autonomous fleet uses to negotiate space.

## Round 3 — Define it: what SmartPark is

SmartPark is a platform for mapping, managing and monetising parking spaces as intelligent assets.

It starts with a simple public proposition:

**Every parking space is an asset. SmartPark helps communities, councils and property owners make every space work harder.**

It has five operating layers.

### 1. Space Registry

A canonical inventory of parking spaces, including public, private, commercial, residential, event, roadside, loading, accessible, EV, fleet, permit and temporary spaces.

### 2. Asset Intelligence

Each space becomes a record with location, owner, rules, capacity, demand, turnover, pricing potential, restrictions, monetisation options and community value.

### 3. Market Layer

Spaces can be sold, leased, reserved, bundled, tokenised internally, priced dynamically, included in precinct offers, attached to retail campaigns, or used as logistics and mobility infrastructure.

### 4. Service Layer

Parking officers and enforcement staff shift toward service roles: helping residents, supporting businesses, managing precinct flows, resolving conflicts, assisting vulnerable users, and improving asset performance.

### 5. Autonomous Readiness Layer

The platform prepares cities and property owners for driverless vehicles by creating the rules, locations, interfaces and commercial models required for autonomous vehicle waiting, charging, staging and servicing.

## Round 4 — Make it real: first deployable product

The entry product should not try to boil the ocean.

The first real version is a precinct-level asset audit and monetisation map.

### Product name

SmartPark Asset Map

### First buyer

Councils, shopping precincts, property owners, universities, hospitals, airports, stadiums, and mixed-use developments.

### First deliverable

A mapped inventory of all parking spaces in a defined precinct with an asset score, usage model, revenue model, community impact model and transition plan.

### MVP modules

1. **Spot Inventory** — map every space and classify it.
2. **Rules Register** — capture time limits, permit rules, loading zones, disabled access, EV charging and enforcement rules.
3. **Asset Score** — rank each space by commercial value, community value, turnover value, autonomy readiness and operational risk.
4. **Revenue Options** — identify direct and indirect monetisation paths.
5. **Community Impact** — show effects on local businesses, residents, staff and visitors.
6. **Service Transition Plan** — shift enforcement activity into service, support and productivity roles.
7. **Autonomous Future Map** — identify where driverless vehicles will wait, charge, queue, stage and service.

### Monetisation paths

- precinct audit fee
- monthly asset management subscription
- dynamic pricing layer
- booking/reservation fee
- EV charging partnerships
- fleet staging contracts
- local retailer validation and offers
- event parking optimisation
- data licensing for councils and transport planners
- autonomous vehicle readiness certification
- workforce transition consulting
- parking revenue uplift share
- compliance and accessibility reporting
