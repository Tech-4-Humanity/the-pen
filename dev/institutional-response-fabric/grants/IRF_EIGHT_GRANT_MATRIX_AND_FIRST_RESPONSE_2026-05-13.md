### Project title

Inclusive Community Intelligence Pilot

### Short summary — 220 words

Tech 4 Humanity proposes a small Inclusive Community Intelligence Pilot to help local disability communities identify practical access barriers, support needs and inclusion opportunities in a simple, evidence-backed way.

The pilot will use structured community feedback, plain-English interviews or workshop prompts, and a lightweight accessibility/inclusion review to produce a practical local inclusion report. The focus is not on building a large technology platform. The focus is on listening, organising evidence, and turning community insight into a clear set of actions that local services, families and support organisations can use.

The project will support people with disability, families, carers and local service stakeholders to describe what makes participation harder, what already works, and what small changes would create better access. Outputs will include a short findings report, an inclusion opportunity map, a reusable feedback template, and a simple evidence pack that can support future local planning or funding.

Any examples or demonstration data will be clearly labelled as synthetic. Real participant feedback will only be used with consent and will be reported in a de-identified, respectful and non-clinical way. The project will not provide medical, legal, therapeutic or NDIS eligibility advice. It will focus on community inclusion, accessibility, navigation and practical improvement opportunities.

### Need statement

People with disability, families and carers often experience fragmented information, inconsistent access pathways and avoidable participation barriers. Small local organisations may know some of these issues informally, but they often lack a simple way to collect, organise and present the evidence. This pilot creates a low-cost, practical method for turning lived experience and service observations into clear local inclusion actions.

### Who benefits

- People with disability seeking better local access and participation.
- Families and carers who navigate services and community activities.
- Local service providers wanting clearer feedback.
- Community organisations needing evidence for future improvements.
- Council or funders seeking practical, grounded inclusion insights.

### Safeguarding and governance note

The pilot is informational and community-focused. It does not provide diagnosis, treatment, crisis advice, legal interpretation, NDIS eligibility guarantees or funding promises. Participant input will be voluntary, consent-based and de-identified in reporting unless explicit permission is provided. Synthetic examples may be used only for demonstration and will be labelled.
