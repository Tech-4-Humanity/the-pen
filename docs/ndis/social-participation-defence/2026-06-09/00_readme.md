# NDIS Social Participation Defence Pack v1

Generated: 2026-06-09

## Purpose

This pack converts the NDIS social/community participation reform collision into a practical product asset.

The policy collision:

- social, civic and community participation budgets are under direct reduction pressure;
- functional-capacity evidence becomes more important from 2028;
- providers must claim faster and keep better records;
- families risk losing supports without structured evidence of function, independence, inclusion and risk reduction.

## Product position
