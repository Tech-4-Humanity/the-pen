# House Rule — Clockworks Research Row Evaluation

Status: ACTIVE

Any research study, subtopic, novel idea, hypothesis, signal, role, machine, place, technology, policy change, business activity, or conversation discovery must be treated as a Clockwork candidate.

Required flow:
Research Row -> Universal Impact Matrix -> Scoring Engine -> Visual Diagnostic -> Evidence Status -> Next Action.

Required dimensions:
CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, CHRO, CMO, CRO, Risk, Strategy, Machines, Signal, Places, Dr Doolittle.

Survivability rule:
Adding a new study, subtopic, idea, signal, machine, role, human factor, or business context must not require architecture redesign.

Visual rule:
The cube, diagram, cartoon, dodecahedron, or dashboard is a diagnostic view only.

Evidence rule:
No row is REAL without evidence.

Conversation codification rule:
Important discoveries, examples, metaphors, evaluation logic, scoring logic, interaction patterns, and implementation implications must be written to a system of record before close.

Anti-patterns:
- flattening Clockworks into a static taxonomy
- treating the cube as the architecture
- losing examples from conversation
- claiming completion without receipts
- leaving session discoveries uncommitted
