Payroll + Travel + Outlook + MYOB

Prototype view for a staff travel, hotel choice, payment transparency, reimbursement, and MYOB export system. Outlook is the employee front door. MYOB is the finance and payroll integration layer. Raw card storage is blocked.

Operating model

1. OutlookEmployee sees flights, hotels, receipts, approvals and actions inside Outlook.
2. Travel PortalTrip record, hotel selection, policy checks and exception handling.
3. Payment VaultTokenised card references only. No raw card numbers or CVV.
4. MYOB AdapterReimbursement, spend and cost-centre export with reconciliation.
5. Audit LedgerEvery approval, payment, override, export and exception receives a receipt.
