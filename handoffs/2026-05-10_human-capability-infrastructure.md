## Category Statement

Tech 4 Humanity is building **Human Capability Infrastructure**: an AI-orchestrated ecosystem that enables personalised learning, adaptive support, longitudinal evidence, and lifelong augmentation across education, workforce, community, and human development systems.

---

## Strategic Thesis

Competitors compete on apps, prompts, worksheets, tutoring workflows, and resource marketplaces. The stronger move is infrastructure.

The moat is not content. It is:
- Signal
- Consent
- Orchestration
- Capability memory
- Evidence
- Adaptive pathways
- Outcome telemetry
- Teacher/provider monetisation
- Government-grade reporting
- Lifelong augmentation

---

## Core Promise
