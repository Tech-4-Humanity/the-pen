## Operating correction

Outcome Ready is for:
- participants
- practitioners
- providers

Families, schools, pharmacies, and other partners are important channels and support actors, but the site should not collapse into a parent-only, pharmacy-only, or medical-product story.

## Site positioning

Outcome Ready is the practical support operating layer for participants, practitioners, and providers.

It turns everyday signals into structured support evidence, practical activities, reports, and progress tracking.

It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace qualified professional care.
