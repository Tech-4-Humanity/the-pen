## 1. Product Name

# CTO in Your Pocket

A low-noise, self-healing operating layer for founders, small businesses, lean operators, and AI-enabled teams who need CTO-grade awareness without enterprise dashboards, ticket theatre, or expensive standing teams.

The customer-facing promise:

> Your business has a CTO watching the things that matter, fixing what can be fixed, and only interrupting you when action is genuinely required.

The internal engine:

> Solo CTO Control Layer — detect → decide → remediate → validate → log → escalate.

## 5. Buyer Stories

### Story 1 — The Solo Founder

A founder launches a product on Vercel, takes payments through Stripe, captures leads into Supabase, and uses AI agents to handle intake and follow-up.

Old way:

- They only know something is broken when a customer complains.
- They check dashboards randomly.
- They waste time wondering what failed.
- They cannot tell whether it was the form, the database, Stripe, or the automation.

CTO in Your Pocket:

- Checks the website every few minutes.
- Submits a synthetic test lead.
- Opens the Stripe checkout link.
- Checks the queue heartbeat.
- Re-runs failed safe jobs.
- Logs recovery evidence.
- Sends a short message only if recovery fails.

Outcome:

The founder is not managing IT. The founder is managing exceptions.

---

### Story 2 — The Small Business Operator

A trades business, clinic, tutor, NDIS provider, or agency has a website, booking form, payment link, inbox, CRM, and quote process.

Old way:

- A missed form equals a missed job.
- A broken booking link equals lost revenue.
- No one checks until the business owner notices fewer leads.
- The tech stack is too small for enterprise support but too important to ignore.

CTO in Your Pocket:

- Watches the quote form.
- Confirms submissions are landing.
- Checks booking/payment flow.
- Creates a fallback task if the normal route fails.
- Sends a plain-English escalation: “Booking flow failed. Fallback capture enabled. Three customers affected. Action needed.”

Outcome:

The business gets operational resilience without hiring a CTO.

---

### Story 3 — The AI-Enabled Organisation

A lean organisation starts using agents, automations, and AI workflows across research, reporting, customer support, content, and operations.

Old way:

- Agents silently stall.
- Reports fail without visibility.
- Model/API costs drift upward.
- People assume the AI is working because no one says otherwise.

CTO in Your Pocket:

- Watches agent heartbeats.
- Tracks workflow completion.
- Detects repeated recovery patterns.
- Flags cost anomalies.
- Classifies claims as REAL, PARTIAL, or PRETEND based on evidence.

Outcome:

The organisation can scale AI without pretending the machines are always fine.

## 8. Product Modules

### Pocket Watch

Basic monitoring of important assets.

Includes:

- Website uptime
- API endpoint checks
- Form availability
- Stripe link open check
- Queue heartbeat

### Pocket Fix

Safe remediation.

Includes:

- Retry job
- Re-run report
- Requeue failed task
- Trigger fallback form
- Restart safe worker
- Switch to backup path where configured

### Pocket Proof

Validation and evidence.

Includes:

- Validate that fix worked
- Log incident
- Attach proof
- Mark REAL/PARTIAL/PRETEND
- Surface repeated weak spots

### Pocket Nudge

Low-noise escalation.

Includes:

- Plain-English alert
- Impact summary
- Attempted fixes
- Recommended next action
- Approval request for gated actions

### Pocket Review

Operating intelligence.

Includes:

- Weekly summary
- Monthly pattern report
- Top risks
- Repeated recoveries
- Cost anomalies
- Suggested hardening work

## 15. FAQ

### Is this managed IT?

No. It is a low-noise control layer for the key workflows that keep your business alive.

### Does it replace a CTO?

It does not replace strategic leadership, but it gives small operators CTO-grade visibility, recovery, and escalation for common operational failures.

### Will it stop all outages?

No. It reduces surprise, shortens recovery, and escalates only when automation cannot solve the problem.

### Do I need to understand the technology?

No. The system reports in business language: what failed, what was tried, what recovered, and what action is required.

### What makes this different from uptime monitoring?

Uptime monitoring tells you something is down. CTO in Your Pocket attempts safe recovery, validates the result, logs evidence, and escalates only if recovery fails.
