- One **product spotlight** within that brand

## Anti-Drift Rules

- **One dominant brand per week.** Never mix Biz and Kids in the same major post.
- **Spotlight one product per week.** Avoid menu-fatigue.
- **Cite sources for reform claims.** Always link to official source.
- **No diagnostic language in Kids content.** Functional + participation + outcomes only.
- **No "buy now or your business dies" fear-mongering in Biz content.** Urgency + facts only.
- **Always have a free entry product visible.** Snapshot + Alert.
