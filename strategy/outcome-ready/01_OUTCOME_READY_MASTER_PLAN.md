# 01 — Outcome Ready Master Plan (May → Dec 2026)

## 1. Business Identity

**Outcome Ready** = the master business under Tech 4 Humanity Pty Ltd (ABN 70 666 271 272).

Outcome Ready does one thing:

> Turn pressure into outcomes, with evidence.

Where there is regulatory pressure, system change, or operational chaos, Outcome Ready provides:
- **Decision** (assessment, pathway, classification)
- **Execution** (structured intervention, agent-led work)
- **Evidence** (defensible proof of what happened and what changed)

Not therapy. Not consulting. Not generic AI tools. **Outcome infrastructure under pressure.**

## 2. Brand Hierarchy

```
Tech 4 Humanity Pty Ltd
    └── Outcome Ready (master business)
            ├── Thriving Biz   (B2B provider survival + operations)
            └── Thriving Kids  (consumer/family + school + practitioner)
```

Same underlying engine. Different audiences. Different language. Different timing.

### Why two brands, not one

- Buyers feel different pressure
- Buying triggers happen at different times (July reforms vs October Thriving Kids rollout)
- Mixing messages dilutes both
- Separation enables cleaner campaigns, pricing, and partner channels

## 4. The Two Windows

### Window 1 — May → July 2026 — Thriving Biz dominant

**Trigger:** Mandatory provider registration expansion (SIL + platforms) begins 1 July 2026. Increased compliance enforcement, digital claiming, audit activity.

**Buyer state:** Anxious, time-pressured, looking for survival tools.

**Sales motion:** Urgency-led. Short cycle. Direct outreach + LinkedIn authority.

**Revenue mix target:** 70% Thriving Biz / 25% Thriving Kids seeding / 5% other.

### Window 2 — August → October 2026 — Thriving Kids dominant

**Trigger:** Thriving Kids rollout begins 1 October 2026. Parents already seeing NDIS plan reductions for low/moderate-needs children. Confusion peak.

**Buyer state:** Confused, fearful of losing support, seeking clarity.

**Sales motion:** Emotion-led, education-led. Slower cycles but higher volume.

**Revenue mix target:** 60% Thriving Kids / 30% Thriving Biz steady-state / 10% other.

### Window 3 — November → December 2026 — Both run

**Trigger:** Reform settles, market segments stabilise. Buyers move from urgency to system selection.

**Buyer state:** Comparing options, committing to ongoing systems.

**Sales motion:** Retention + expansion. Multi-product bundles. Annual contracts.

**Revenue mix target:** 45% Biz / 45% Kids / 10% adjacent sectors (accountants, allied health).

## 6. Strategic Principles (Non-Negotiable)

1. **Same engine, different skin.** Do not build new agents per market — repackage existing capability.
2. **Pressure-aligned messaging.** Lead with what the buyer is feeling, not with the tech.
3. **Evidence is the moat.** Every product must generate defensible artefacts.
4. **Functional, not diagnostic.** Avoid diagnosis-based positioning; lean into function, participation, outcomes.
5. **No clinical claims.** Avoid medical advice, diagnostic claims, or eligibility guarantees.
6. **Loop everything back.** Usage → signals → profile updates → improved recommendations. Living system, not static tool.
7. **Distribute via attention windows.** Promote what the market is ready to hear, when they are ready to hear it.
8. **Rotate within each brand.** Inside each window, rotate product spotlight weekly to avoid fatigue.

## 7. What This Plan Replaces

This plan replaces:
- Ad-hoc product launches
- One-off campaigns
- Mixed-audience messaging
- "AI platform" generic positioning
- Diagnosis-led / NDIS-only positioning

This plan does not replace:
- Existing T4H infrastructure commitments
- The canonical doctrine in PEN (`NDIS_THRIVING_KIDS_OPERATING_DOCTRINE_*.md`)
- Compliance and governance obligations (BAS, Div7A, RDTI, etc.)

## 8. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|-----------|
| Policy reverses or delays | Brands and products remain useful across non-NDIS sectors (accountants, allied health, education) |
| Parent backlash against "AI in childcare" | Lead with parent control, evidence, and choice. Avoid "AI replaces therapist" framing |
| Provider hesitation to adopt | Lead with risk reduction (audit defence) before efficiency |
| Over-promising eligibility | Never claim NDIS eligibility outcomes. Position as evidence + decision support |
| Two brands dilute focus | Calendar-locked windows enforce single-brand focus per period |
| Confusing Reading Buddy with the whole system | Always frame Reading Buddy as "AI Sweet Spots applied to reading" |
