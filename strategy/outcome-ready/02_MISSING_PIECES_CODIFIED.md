## Why These Matter Together

These are not 15 separate ideas. They form one coherent operating model:

```
Pressure (reform / change)
    ↓
Attention (windows when buyers listen)
    ↓
Brand (Thriving Biz or Thriving Kids)
    ↓
Audience (3 markets per brand)
    ↓
AI Sweet Spots (assess & route)
    ↓
Agents (do the work — same agents, different skin)
    ↓
Application layer (Reading Buddy if education, others as needed)
    ↓
Outcome Ready (prove, defend, escalate)
    ↓
Signals (loop back to improve the system)
```

If you build the engine once and skin it twice with the right timing, you sell into two markets with one capability stack.

## What Must NOT Drift

- Reading Buddy must not become "the platform" — it is a specialist app.
- AI Sweet Spots must not become "just a quiz" — it is the routing engine.
- Thriving Biz must not become "just compliance" — compliance is the wedge.
- Thriving Kids must not become "just autism" — it is developmental + functional.
- Outcome Ready must not become "another SaaS" — it is outcome + evidence infrastructure.

## Compliance Guardrails

- No diagnostic claims.
- No NDIS eligibility guarantees.
- No clinical advice in product copy.
- No targeting of minors with sales messaging.
- Functional language, not medical language, in family-facing material.
- Provider-facing material must reflect actual published rules, not assumptions.
- Reform Alert content must cite source policy/news, not speculate.
