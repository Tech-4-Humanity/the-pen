# 03 — Thriving Biz Product Catalogue

## Brand Promise

> Stay compliant. Stay operational. Run your business with agents.

Compliance is the wedge. Operations is the bigger play. Both are sold under one brand.

## Audience

| Layer | Who | Pressure |
|-------|-----|----------|
| Core | The business itself | Survival, revenue, compliance |
| Decision | Owner / operator | "Am I ready for July?" |
| Delivery | Practitioner / staff | Admin overload, reporting load |
| System | Provider entity | Audits, registration, claims |
| Oversight | Gov / ATO / NDIA / accountant | External enforcement layer |

Same business often plays multiple roles (a sole-trader therapist is owner + practitioner + provider).

## Compliance Posture

Thriving Biz must:
- Never claim NDIA certification or endorsement we don't have
- Never guarantee audit outcomes
- Always reflect current published NDIS rules (with sources)
- Provide tools, not legal/accounting advice
- Be clear that providers remain responsible for their own compliance decisions
