# 05 — Audience & Role Map

The same person can appear in multiple layers across both brands. The system recognises them once via Identity + ConsentX, then surfaces the right brand experience for the role they are playing in that session.

## Thriving Kids — Roles

| Layer | Who | What they feel | What they want | Entry product |
|-------|-----|----------------|----------------|---------------|
| Core | The child / participant | Wants to be understood, engaged | Engaging experience, no jargon | Reading Buddy (child UI) |
| Decision | Parent | Confused, anxious, protective | Clarity, control, evidence | AI Sweet Spots → Parent Action Plan |
| Delivery | Practitioner | Overworked, reporting fatigue | Less admin, defensible outputs | Practitioner Reporting Pack |
| System | Provider | Transitioning, margin pressure | Operate across NDIS + Foundational Supports | Foundational Supports Transition Pack |
| Scale | School | Overloaded, no structured tools | Student profiles, classroom strategies, reports | Student Support Pack + 2e Toolkit |

## Thriving Biz — Roles

| Layer | Who | What they feel | What they want | Entry product |
|-------|-----|----------------|----------------|---------------|
| Core | The business | Survival pressure | Stay operating, stay paid | Provider Risk + Compliance Scan |
| Decision | Owner / operator | Time-pressured, anxious about July | Survival roadmap | Reform Readiness Snapshot → Scan |
| Delivery | Practitioner / staff | Drowning in admin | Auto notes, calls, intake | AI Office + AI Calls |
| System | Provider entity | Audits looming, claims scrutiny | Compliance OS + Evidence Engine | Compliance OS |
| Oversight | Gov / NDIA / ATO / accountant | External enforcement | Reliable provider partner | Audit Defence Pack |

Government and ATO are not buyers in the normal sense, but the oversight layer drives buyer behaviour. Accountants are a separate channel partner and can resell.

## Cross-Brand Identity

A single human can be:
- A parent (Thriving Kids — Decision layer)
- A speech pathologist running a sole-trader practice (Thriving Biz — Owner, Practitioner, Provider all in one)
- A school teacher (Thriving Kids — Scale layer)

The platform must:
1. Recognise the same person across roles via Identity
2. Get consent per role via ConsentX
3. Surface only the relevant brand experience at any given moment
4. Avoid bleed-through (do not promote Thriving Biz to someone in parent mode, and vice versa)

## Messaging Discipline

| Audience | Language to use | Language to avoid |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Parent | child, support, progress, next steps, evidence, your choice | diagnosis, eligibility guaranteed, AI replaces therapist, NDIS hack |
| Practitioner | reduce admin, defensible reports, outcomes you can show, time back | "automate yourself out of a job", "AI therapist" |
| Provider | stay registered, audit-ready, claim defensible, operational continuity | "NDIS will fail you", "compliance loophole" |
| School | structured support, complex students, evidence-ready, capacity | "fix the kid", "replace special ed" |
| Owner (Biz) | survive July, stay paid, run with agents, less admin more delivery | "AI takeover", "fire your staff" |

## Conversion Patterns

### Parent
1. Sees post about NDIS / Thriving Kids changes
2. Takes free Family Risk Snapshot
3. Buys AI Sweet Spots child profile ($29–79)
4. Joins Parent OS subscription ($19–49/mo)
5. Buys Reading Buddy as add-on
6. Buys Plan Defence / Escalation Pack at moment of need

### Practitioner
1. Follows Troy on LinkedIn for reform commentary
2. Downloads Reform Readiness Snapshot (free)
3. Adopts Practitioner Reporting Pack ($49–149/mo)
4. Recommends Reading Buddy to families in caseload
5. Becomes a referral source for Thriving Biz Practice OS bundle

### Provider
1. Sees urgency post about July
2. Books Provider Risk + Compliance Scan
3. Buys Audit Defence Pack or Registration Readiness Pack
4. Subscribes to Compliance OS + Evidence Engine
5. Expands into AI Office / Calls
6. Bundles into Full Stack Biz

### School
1. Direct outreach or referral from a parent customer
2. Pilot one classroom (10–20 students)
3. Renew on annual licence + 2e Toolkit
4. Scale across year levels

## Anti-Pattern Map

| Anti-pattern | Why bad |
|--------------|---------|
| Selling Thriving Biz to a parent | Wrong pressure, wrong language, breaks trust |
| Selling Thriving Kids to a sole-trader provider | They have business survival pressure, not family pressure |
| Showing child-friendly Reading Buddy UI to a provider | Looks toy-like for a B2B buyer |
| Pitching "AI" first | Buyers do not buy AI; they buy outcomes |
| Pitching the platform | Sell the SKU that solves the specific pressure |
| One landing page for everyone | Always split per audience |
