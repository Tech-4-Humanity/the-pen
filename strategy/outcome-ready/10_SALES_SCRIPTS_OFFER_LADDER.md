# 10 — Sales Scripts & Offer Ladder

## Offer Ladder Logic

```
FREE LEAD MAGNET → LOW-COST ENTRY PRODUCT → CORE SUBSCRIPTION → HIGH-TICKET DEFENSIVE / OPERATIONAL → BUNDLE / ENTERPRISE
```

Every audience moves through this ladder. Steps may be skipped under urgency.

### Conversation playbook (Provider, post-snapshot, red score)

**Three questions:**
1. "What does your current evidence trail look like — session notes, outcomes, or just invoices?"
2. "If you got a Commission notice tomorrow, how long would it take you to produce 30 days of evidence?"
3. "How much of your week is going to admin you can't bill for?"

**Offer:** Compliance Scan ($X) OR Compliance OS subscription.

### Audit Notice received — high urgency

"Stop. Don't respond yet. Three things:
1. Don't make any new claim entries until we look at what you have.
2. Pull together the last 90 days of session records, claims, and any outcome notes.
3. Book the Audit Defence Pack — we'll have a defensible response ready in [X] days."

## Conversion Triggers Map

| Trigger | First product to offer | Why |
|---------|------------------------|-----|
| Cold contact, no signal | Free Snapshot | Build list |
| Snapshot completed, low risk | Newsletter / nurture | Not ready |
| Snapshot completed, high risk | Scan / Sweet Spots paid | Ready |
| Audit notice received | Audit Defence Pack | Highest urgency |
| Plan cut received | Plan Defence Pack | Highest urgency |
| Subscription started | Cross-sell adjacent SKU at day 14 | Onboarding hook |
| 30 days no activity | Re-engagement | Save churn |
| 90 days subscribed, healthy | Pitch bundle / enterprise | Expansion |

## Objection Handling — Top 10

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "I already have a CRM / practice management tool" | "Different layer. Yours runs the practice. We run the evidence + compliance + reform-tracking layer." |
| "Isn't this just AI hype?" | "Every output has a source, a timestamp, and an audit trail." |
| "What if Thriving Kids gets delayed again?" | "Our products are still useful. Functional tracking, evidence, parent navigation don't depend on dates." |
| "I don't want AI making decisions about my child" | "AI Sweet Spots produces a profile and a pathway *option*. You decide." |
| "Will this guarantee my NDIS access?" | "No. Nothing can. We produce the strongest evidence the system will accept." |
| "I'm not tech-savvy" | "Answer questions, get a report. No setup, no jargon." |
| "How is this different from [competitor]?" | "We work across the transition AND we generate audit-defensible evidence." |
| "Sounds expensive" | "Compared to one audit failure or one lost month of support — no." |
| "Why should I trust you on this?" | "We publish our sources, we cite policy directly, and we tell you when we don't know." |
| "I'll think about it" | "All good. Want me to send the Snapshot link?" |

## Anti-Patterns (Do Not)

- Do not pitch in the first message
- Do not promise eligibility outcomes
- Do not use scarcity ("only 3 spots left")
- Do not use fake urgency
- Do not target parents during evening hours
- Do not contact children directly, ever
- Do not bring up sensitive past plan reductions unless the user mentions them first
- Do not send the same message twice in 7 days
