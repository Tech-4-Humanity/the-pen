## Collapsed Into Four Core Functions

All 20 opportunities collapse into:

1. **Assess / classify** (Sweet Spots)
2. **Guide / intervene** (Agents + Reading Buddy)
3. **Track / measure** (Signals + LifeGraph)
4. **Prove / escalate** (Outcome Ready Evidence Engine)

These are the four engine functions. Everything else is packaging.
