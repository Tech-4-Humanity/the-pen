# 04 — Thriving Kids Product Catalogue

## Brand Promise

> Understand your child. Support their progress. Prove what is working.

Not a therapy service. Not a diagnostic tool. A decision + execution + evidence system for families navigating a changing support landscape.

## Audience (Three Markets + Scale Layer)

| Layer | Who | Pressure |
|-------|-----|----------|
| Core | The child / participant | Engagement, outcomes (indirect buyer) |
| Decision | Parent / family | "What do I do? Will we lose support?" |
| Delivery | Practitioner (therapist, educator, specialist) | Reporting load, outcome pressure |
| System | Provider (clinic, allied health business) | Transition NDIS → Foundational Supports |
| Scale | School / early learning centre | Capacity overload, complex students |

## Specialist App — Reading Buddy

Reading Buddy is the first specialist application built on the engine. Not the platform.

- **For:** Children aged 5–12 needing structured reading support at home or school
- **Powered by:** AI Sweet Spots profile + adaptive difficulty + daily engagement
- **Generates:** Reading time, error patterns, comprehension signals, disengagement points
- **Feeds:** Functional Progress Tracker + Evidence packs

## Twice Exceptional (2e) Pathway

Explicit. Not buried.

- **Entry:** AI Sweet Spots flags 2e pattern
- **Routes to:** 2e Family Bundle / School 2e Toolkit / Reading Buddy with 2e tuning
- **Positioning:** "Not underperforming. Misaligned."

## Compliance Posture

Thriving Kids must:
- Never diagnose
- Never claim to replace clinical care
- Never claim NDIS or Thriving Kids eligibility outcomes
- Always describe functional indicators, not medical conditions
- Always present pathway options, not decisions
- Always require parent consent for child data
- Use ConsentX as the consent and permission layer
- Be age-appropriate in all child-facing UI (Reading Buddy in particular)
- Never display sales messaging directed at children
