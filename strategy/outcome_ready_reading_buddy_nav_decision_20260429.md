# Outcome Ready Navigation Decision: Reading Buddy

Date: 2026-04-29

## Decision

Reading Buddy should come off the top-level Outcome Ready navigation.

Outcome Ready top-level positioning is for:
- participants
- practitioners
- providers

Reading Buddy remains important, but it is not the top-line brand spine. It should sit as one educational product under the broader Outcome Ready product family, with its own dedicated space.

## Rationale

Reading Buddy will become one of several educational products. Keeping it in the top line risks making Outcome Ready look like a literacy-only site, which is too narrow.

Reading Buddy also needs broader positioning than children only. It should cover:
- children
- adults
- neurodiverse learners
- families
- practitioners supporting reading and comprehension
- providers running learning support programs

## Site implication

Top-level navigation should prioritise:
- Participants
- Practitioners
- Providers
- How it works
- Pricing
- Reports / Evidence

Reading Buddy should move to:
- Products section
- Education products hub
- Dedicated /reading-buddy page

## Future discussion item

After the initial Outcome Ready site discussions are complete, review Reading Buddy as its own product space:
- audience model
- child/adult/neurodiverse language
- education product family relationship
- pricing
- practitioner/provider use
- report outputs
- integration with NeuroProfile and Activity Engine

## Guardrail

Do not delete Reading Buddy. Reposition it.

Do not make Outcome Ready subordinate to Reading Buddy.
