# Outcome Ready Site Rework: Aligning to ThrivingOS Loop and Product Spine

Date: 2026-04-29
Source site: https://outcome-ready.vercel.app
Pages to rework:
- /pricing
- /how-it-works
- /families
Reference visual: ThrivingOS section showing continuous signal, cross-environment support, family-visible evidence, and funding-ready outputs.

## Decision

The reference visual matches the strategic direction better than the current Outcome Ready wedge language, but it must be adapted.

Use the ThrivingOS loop as the operating model:
1. signals captured
2. patterns processed
3. actions triggered
4. evidence structured
5. progress recognised

But Outcome Ready must own the product and revenue spine:
- Before the Appointment Pack
- Functional Evidence Pack
- Family Plan / NeuroHub Lite
- Practitioner Console
- Activity Library
- Partner referral channels

ThrivingOS language is strong for system architecture. Outcome Ready language must be clearer for buyers.

## What matches

The visual aligns with Outcome Ready because it says:
- not another parent app
- always-on between sessions
- home, school, therapy, community
- structured evidence
- NDIS and Thriving Kids alignment
- progress can be explained

This supports the NeuroProfile, Support Graph, Functional Evidence Pack, and Support Coordination Agent direction.

## What needs enhancement

The current phrase “An OS for a child’s world” is powerful but too broad for conversion.

Outcome Ready needs:
- a sharper first-action CTA
- explicit products
- clearer pricing ladder
- clear distinction between families, practitioners, schools, providers, and partners
- proof/report examples
- boundary language: not diagnosis, not prescribing, not clinical replacement

## Recommended homepage / family page hero

Headline:
Outcome Ready turns everyday observations into useful support evidence.

Subheadline:
For families, practitioners, schools, and providers navigating attention, autism, learning, regulation, sensory load, NDIS, and Thriving Kids support pathways.

CTA 1:
Start a Before the Appointment Pack

CTA 2:
See a sample Functional Evidence Report

Trust line:
Outcome Ready does not diagnose or prescribe. It helps people organise evidence, run practical support activities, and track functional progress with professional care where needed.

## Reworked how-it-works page

Use five sections:

1. Capture signals
Everyday examples from home, school, work, service settings, activities, routines, and family observations.

2. Map patterns
Outcome Ready maps observations to functional domains such as attention, executive function, learning access, communication, sensory load, regulation, and AI support needs.

3. Choose activities
Families and practitioners select practical activities from the NeuroActivity Library.

4. Build evidence
Each activity and observation becomes structured evidence for reports, handoffs, school meetings, provider reviews, and support planning.

5. Show progress
Reports show what changed, what helped, what is still stuck, and what should happen next.

## Above-the-fold test

A new visitor should understand within 10 seconds:
- what this is
- who it is for
- what to buy first
- that it is not a diagnostic or prescribing service
- what output they receive

If not, the page fails.
