## Core expansion

Outcome Ready should become a one-stop support layer for people, families, practitioners, schools, providers, workplaces, and partner channels.

It should help people before, during, and after professional care by organising evidence, supporting practical activities, guiding conversations, and tracking functional outcomes.

Outcome Ready is not a replacement for qualified professional care.

## Market problem

The current support journey is fragmented:
- people do not know where to start
- families carry the coordination burden
- schools and workplaces need practical supports
- practitioners need structured evidence and repeatable activities
- providers need outcome reports
- partner channels need safe referral paths
- support records are scattered and hard to reuse

Outcome Ready can become the connective tissue.
