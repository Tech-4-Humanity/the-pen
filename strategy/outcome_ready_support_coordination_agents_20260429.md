# Outcome Ready Support Coordination Agents

Date: 2026-04-29
Repo: TML-4PM/the-pen
Status: Strategy and build blueprint
Reality status: PARTIAL until deployed, tested, and receipt-bound

## Executive direction

Outcome Ready needs its own products and services. Partner channels are useful, but they must not become the strategy.

All Chemist and similar sites can be touchpoints, referral surfaces, content hosts, and partner channels. They are not the product centre.

The product centre is Outcome Ready: functional support, readiness, coordination, activities, reporting, evidence, and longitudinal outcomes.

## The missing capability

A support coordination agent layer is vital.

People do not only need content. They need help moving through a fragmented journey:
- finding what matters
- preparing for professional conversations
- collecting evidence
- understanding support options
- coordinating parents, schools, practitioners, providers, workplaces, and partners
- tracking activities
- proving whether support is working
- knowing when to escalate to a qualified professional

## Agent architecture

### 1. Intake Navigator Agent

Purpose:
Guide people from uncertainty into a structured support pathway.

Responsibilities:
- collect safe background information
- explain boundaries
- classify current stage of journey
- identify immediate support needs
- generate readiness checklist
- route to relevant product pack

Outputs:
- readiness summary
- journey stage
- recommended support pathway
- next safe action

### 2. Evidence Pack Agent

Purpose:
Turn scattered observations into structured evidence.

Responsibilities:
- collect examples from home, school, work, and daily life
- group observations by functional domain
- prepare appointment-ready summaries
- create handoff notes
- preserve source and confidence

Outputs:
- evidence pack
- functional friction snapshot
- professional handoff brief

### 3. Parent and Carer Agent

Purpose:
Support the people doing the daily coordination work.

Responsibilities:
- create home routines
- create school conversation scripts
- track stress and load
- suggest safe support activities
- build family evidence journal
- coordinate multiple children or participants where applicable

Outputs:
- parent action plan
- school meeting pack
- family routine pack
- weekly support summary

### 4. Practitioner Enablement Agent

Purpose:
Help practitioners and providers use Outcome Ready outputs efficiently.

Responsibilities:
- summarise profiles for professional review
- suggest activity packs by functional need
- prepare follow-up templates
- track before and after outcomes
- produce practice-ready reports

Outputs:
- practitioner summary
- activity recommendation pack
- progress note draft
- outcome report

### 5. Support Coordinator Agent

Purpose:
Act as the central coordination layer across people, supports, providers, and evidence.

Responsibilities:
- maintain support graph
- track who is involved
- track commitments and actions
- detect gaps, duplicated effort, stalled actions, and missing evidence
- trigger follow-ups
- create plain-language summaries

Outputs:
- support graph
- action register
- stakeholder map
- gap report
- coordination summary

### 6. School and Learning Agent

Purpose:
Turn functional patterns into school-ready supports.

Responsibilities:
- map learning friction
- suggest classroom supports
- create teacher observation prompts
- support reading and attention activities
- produce school meeting briefs

Outputs:
- school support plan
- teacher observation template
- learning access report

### 7. Workplace Function Agent

Purpose:
Support adults at work without forcing disclosure or diagnosis claims.

Responsibilities:
- map task friction
- suggest executive-function scaffolds
- create communication repair scripts
- create meeting and workload supports
- tune AI support intensity

Outputs:
- work function profile
- adjustment-light support plan
- AI scaffold plan

### 8. Partner Channel Agent

Purpose:
Manage partner surfaces without over-pivoting the business.

Responsibilities:
- manage partner content blocks
- keep partner-safe wording
- generate referral links
- track attribution
- flag stale partner material

Outputs:
- partner content pack
- referral campaign pack
- attribution report

### 9. Resource Scan Agent

Purpose:
Continuously map third-party resources and opportunities.

Responsibilities:
- catalogue public resources
- classify partner targets
- identify reusable patterns
- detect gaps
- avoid copying protected content
- generate resource registry entries

Outputs:
- resource registry
- asset pattern log
- partner target list
- market gap report

### 10. Reality and Safety Agent

Purpose:
Prevent overclaiming and keep the system honest.

Responsibilities:
- classify claims as REAL, PARTIAL, or PRETEND
- ensure evidence exists
- check boundary language
- flag risky wording
- require human review where needed

Outputs:
- evidence ledger entries
- risk flags
- review queue
- proof status

## Support graph model

Track:
- participant or user reference
- parent or carer role
- practitioner role
- provider role
- school role
- workplace role
- partner channel role
- activities assigned
- evidence contributed
- support actions due
- outcomes observed
- escalation status

This is the core moat. Content can be copied. A live support graph with evidence, activities, and outcomes is much harder to copy.

The next layer is not another page, another partner, or another campaign. It is the support coordination layer.
