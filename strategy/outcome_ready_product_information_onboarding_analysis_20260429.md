# 1. Source A Analysis: Agentic Product Spec and Portfolio Control Layer

## What it contains

Source A defines a reusable Agentic Product Spec (APS v1.0). It frames every product as an executable object with:

- product identity
- 30-second customer contract
- core measurable outcome
- agent task map
- pre-sale, sale, use, and post-sale lifecycle
- system requirements
- data structure
- proof and Reality Ledger classification
- revenue engine
- failure and recovery
- distribution priority

It then adds a Portfolio Control Layer (PCL v1.0) to stop product sprawl by forcing:

- market category
- target segment
- price band
- direct and indirect competitors
- differentiation axis
- internal collision detection
- portfolio role
- product distance scoring
- pricing alignment
- roadmap alignment
- kill, merge, reposition, or limit-investment decisions

## Strongest idea

The strongest idea is that no product should be allowed to exist unless it passes both:

1. a customer/execution spec
2. a portfolio collision check

This is the missing governance layer. Without it, Outcome Ready will keep spawning overlapping products that confuse buyers and waste build effort.

## Most valuable reusable components

- APS v1.0 as canonical product spec
- PCL v1.0 as mandatory portfolio gate
- overlap_score and distance_score
- role types: ENTRY, CORE, EXPANSION, PREMIUM, EXPERIMENT
- required proof classification: REAL, PARTIAL, PRETEND
- Supabase product_portfolio table concept

## Risks or gaps

- The APS is strong but still document-first unless enforced by the bridge.
- Product distance scoring needs a real scoring method, not subjective labels.
- Pricing alignment needs to link to Stripe and real conversion data.
- Roadmap alignment must be automated or it will be forgotten.

## Decision

Adopt APS plus PCL as the mandatory product governance standard for Outcome Ready and then later for the wider T4H portfolio.

The strongest idea is that Outcome Ready is not an ADHD app, autism app, pharmacy app, or diagnosis app.

That positioning is right.

## Most valuable reusable components

- 11-product decision table

- product clusters
- 14-day launch plan

1. Before the Appointment Pack

# 3. Source C Analysis: Product Spec Enforcement Enhancements

## What it contains

Source C identifies the missing enforcement layer. It says the product spec is strong but still passive unless bound to execution.

It adds ten enforcement enhancements:

1. Product-to-agent binding
2. Economic truth
3. hard Reality Ledger binding
4. runtime enforcement hook
5. product deduplication engine
6. lifecycle automation
7. signal integration
8. pre-sale / sale / use / post-sale universal product engine
9. dossier sync
10. kill switch

## Strongest idea

The strongest idea is that the system must be able to say no.

No execution without spec.
No agent without contract.
No product without revenue logic.
No output without proof.

This turns Outcome Ready from strategy into a controlled product economy.

## Most valuable reusable components

- agent_binding block
- economics block
- proof enforcement hard_block
- bridge pre_check and post_check
- deduplication threshold
- lifecycle health rules
- signal_inputs and signal_usage
- dossier sync updates
- kill switch

## Risks or gaps

- The bridge enforcement must actually be implemented, not written down.
- Dossier sync needs a canonical dossier schema.
- Economic truth needs cost and revenue feeds.
- Kill switch scope must be precise and auditable.

## Decision

Adopt Source C as the execution enforcement layer for APS and PCL.

# 4. Combined Analysis

## The combined architecture

Together, the three sources define a full product operating system:

1. APS defines what a product is.
2. PCL decides whether the product should exist.
3. Outcome Ready portfolio defines what products are in scope.
4. Enforcement enhancements ensure products cannot run unless they are bound to agents, proof, economics, and lifecycle control.

This becomes:

Outcome Ready Product Operating System (OR-POS)

## What Outcome Ready is now

Outcome Ready is the business owner for a neuro-support product portfolio.

Its core product identity:

A one-stop readiness, functional evidence, support coordination, activity, reporting, and practitioner enablement platform for families, schools, providers, practitioners, partner channels, and enterprise white-label buyers.

It stays outside diagnosis, prescribing, and treatment advice.

## The correct product hierarchy

### Core spine

- Outcome Ready NeuroHub
- NeuroProfile Activity Engine
- Support Coordination Agents
- Functional Evidence and Reporting Engine

### Wedge products

- Before the Appointment Pack
- Functional Evidence Pack
- NeuroNavigator Mini Course

### Recurring products

- Family Plan / NeuroHub Lite
- NeuroActivity Library

### Scale products

- Practitioner Console
- School Support Desk
- Provider OS

### Channel products

- Pharmacy Referral Kit
- Partner Referral Kit

### Enterprise products

- White-label NeuroHub
- PHN / provider / clinic / school network rollout

## The biggest strategic insight

Outcome Ready should own the missing middle:

- before formal care
- while waiting
- between professional appointments
- between family, school, provider, practitioner, and partner channels
- after plans are created but before outcomes are visible

That is where families feel lost and systems waste time.

## The biggest operational insight

Reports are not by-products. Reports are products.

The wedge product should produce a report that:

- a parent understands
- a practitioner respects
- a school can use
- a provider can act on
- the system can measure over time

## The biggest enforcement insight

No product should move from idea to build unless it has:

- APS complete
- PCL pass
- agent binding
- proof events
- economic model
- lifecycle state
- Reality Ledger classification
- owner
- kill/merge/reposition decision path

Purpose: prepare people before professional conversations.

- intake completed
- report generated
- user receives next action

# 10. Discussion and decision

## What is now clear

Outcome Ready should not launch all products equally. It should launch one wedge and one report first.

The wedge:
Before the Appointment Pack plus Functional Evidence Pack.

The report:
A single excellent functional support report.

The retention engine:
Family Plan plus Activity Library.

The scale engine:
Practitioner Console.

The channel layer:
Pharmacy and school referral, lightly wired.

The enterprise layer:
Provider OS and White-label NeuroHub after proof.

## What must not happen

- Do not build enterprise CRM first.
- Do not make pharmacy the centre.
- Do not build 11 products as separate startups.
- Do not use clinical-sounding claims before review.
- Do not let agents execute products without spec and proof.

## What should happen next

1. Create OUTRD_ProductSpec_v1.yaml.
2. Create Supabase schema for product governance.
3. Create specs for the first three products.
4. Create one perfect report template.
5. Create bridge enforcement checklist.
6. Create Reality Ledger receipt for product onboarding.
7. Move all existing Outcome Ready strategy assets under this standard.

These sources combine into a strong operating model.

Outcome Ready becomes the product business.

Before the Appointment Pack and Functional Evidence Pack become the wedge.
The Activity Library and Family Plan create recurring value.
The Practitioner Console creates distribution.
