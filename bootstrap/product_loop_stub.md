# Product Loop Stub

Every product must include lifecycle coverage beyond initial build: storage, bootstrap, distribution, use, evidence, feedback, automation, scaling, governance, recovery, monetisation, replication, and uplift.

Use the nine views for review: signal, consent, governance, orchestration, execution, outcomes, distribution, revenue, and experience.

Terms and conditions are treated as a product because they shape behaviour, trust, risk, customer education, and operations.
