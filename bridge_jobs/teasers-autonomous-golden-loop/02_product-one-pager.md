# Teasers Product One-Pager

## Product
Teasers

## Category
Synal / Browser Agent Runtime / Human Effectiveness Signal Engine

## Product statement
Teasers is not a survey, popup, or standalone feature. It is a continuously learning human-state engine embedded in the browser layer.

## Core question
When is a human actually effective?

## Business problem
Most organisations measure activity after the fact: meetings, tickets, surveys, timesheets, output volume, and complaints. These lagging measures miss the live human conditions that shape quality work, decision-making, recovery, overload, and adoption.

## Product answer
Teasers captures tiny declared signals in the flow of work, enriches them with browser/session/context behaviour, and turns them into a Human Effectiveness Vector that can inform people, teams, leaders, AHC, WorkFamilyAI, HoloOrg, Synal, and Command Centre.

## User experience
A lightweight browser or Synal widget asks one short question at useful moments. The user can answer, snooze, or dismiss. The system adapts cadence and learns over time.

## Example prompts
- Feeling more reactive than planned today?
- Are meetings helping or blocking real work?
- Are you busier than last week, or more interrupted?
- Did movement improve your thinking today?

## Core outputs
- Focus Score
- Reactivity Score
- Load Score
- Control Score
- Recovery Score
- Movement Score
- Effectiveness Score
- Human Effectiveness Vector

## Business value
Teasers helps a business move from static engagement surveys to continuous state learning. It identifies where effectiveness is being created, reduced, or distorted. It supports workforce design, AI adoption, meeting reform, wellbeing insight, change readiness, and human-agent orchestration.
