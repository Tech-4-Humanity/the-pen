# Teasers Scoring Engine Summary

Purpose: convert tiny human-state interactions plus work-context signals into a Human Effectiveness Vector.

Core question: when is a human actually effective?

Inputs:
- declared signal: what the user says
- behaviour signal: tab switching, dwell time, latency, snooze, dismiss
- context signal: time, role, calendar density, tenant policy
- movement/recovery signal: walking, breaks, optional wearables later
- history signal: consistency across time

Outputs:
- Focus Score
- Reactivity Score
- Load Score
- Control Score
- Recovery Score
- Movement Score
- Effectiveness Score
- Human Effectiveness Vector

Scoring principle:
Teasers scores conditions and likely work state. It does not judge employees.

Evidence tiers:
- stated
- observed
- correlated
- inferred
- validated

Anti-overclaim rule:
No clinical, medical, or employment judgment from Teasers alone.

Business value:
The business gets a continuously learning signal graph showing whether work conditions are creating effectiveness or friction. This is more valuable than a survey because it compounds over time and links stated experience to observed work behaviour.
