# Class by Cass — Curriculum Alignment Doctrine

**Status:** CANONICAL
**Scope:** Class by Cass, Reading Buddy, Maths Buddy / Maths Mate, related education products
**Source issue:** [TML-4PM/the-pen#101](https://github.com/TML-4PM/the-pen/issues/101)

## Purpose

Class by Cass should be treated as the **school-curriculum and classroom-resource operating layer**, not the whole education federation.

## Scope of ownership

Class by Cass should reuse and, where appropriate, own the school-facing assets for:

- Reading Buddy V2
- Maths Buddy / Maths Mate
- language learning and second-language classroom activities
- personal development resources
- wellbeing and regulation activities
- classroom action packs
- posters, worksheets, templates, slides and bundles
- teacher-facing tools and school-safe curriculum activities

Class by Cass can support adult education, trades learning, health/science or provider-adjacent topics **only when those topics are converted into school-safe, curriculum-aligned teaching material**. Otherwise those belong under their natural anchors such as Institute for Integrated Humanity, AI4Tradies, Outcome Ready, MedLedger or other specialist businesses.

## Curriculum order of operations

1. **New South Wales curriculum first.**
2. **Australian Curriculum** as the expansion layer.
3. **International curriculum mappings** only after the Australian model is stable.

This applies to:
- posters
- worksheets
- templates
- slide decks
- lesson packs
- classroom activities
- reading resources
- maths resources
- language learning resources
- personal development activities
- wellbeing and regulation supports
- classroom action packs

## Reusable school-resource model

Class by Cass acts as a **reusable school-resource engine**: educational outputs from sibling products (Reading Buddy, Maths Buddy) are mapped to NSW/Australian curriculum, given school-safe framings, and surfaced to classroom audiences through Class by Cass distribution rather than re-built per product.

## Reality state

PARTIAL until curriculum mapping tables exist with NSW-first alignment attached to Class by Cass, Reading Buddy, Maths Buddy/Maths Mate and related school-facing products.

## Pressure flags (avoid)

- Generic education claims without curriculum evidence
- Forcing adult/trades/medical/provider learning into Class by Cass unless converted into school-safe curriculum assets
