# Say It Once Content Pack

This pack contains the commercial copy set for the Say It Once / Intent-to-Execution product concept.

Included files:

- `landing-page-quiet.md` — lower-friction homepage copy
- `landing-page-aggressive.md` — stronger, more provocative homepage copy
- `pricing.md` — exact pricing table and packaging logic
- `outbound-first-10.md` — outreach copy for first ten customers
- `video-script.md` — founder-led video script

## Core positioning

Say It Once is the layer between what leaders say and what reality does.

It turns one decision into aligned, role-specific, tool-native execution across teams, systems, partners, and workflows.

## Core audiences

1. Small business and operator-led teams
2. SMB and mid-market organisations
3. Multi-party enterprise and networked ecosystems

Render one of the landing page variants as a dedicated page and keep the rest as campaign and sales assets.
