1 - Future snapshot

Today

Project Managers spend too much time chasing updates, building status packs, maintaining schedules, cleaning RAID logs and translating noise into governance.

2030

Most project administration is agent-assisted or automated. The valuable Project Manager becomes a delivery intelligence operator and outcome assurance leader.

Core shift

From task coordination to outcome orchestration. Less status chasing. More judgement, stakeholder trust and risk ownership.
