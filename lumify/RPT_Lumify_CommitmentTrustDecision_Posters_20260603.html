Infographic 11 · The Trust Map

Owner Trust

Confidence in the CEO to protect value and not create new risk.

Measure: candour + delivery

Board Trust

Confidence in reporting, controls, financial grip and escalation.

Measure: no surprises

Leadership Trust

Confidence that direction is clear and execution is not theatre.

Measure: follow-through

Staff Trust

Confidence that integration will not destroy identity or credibility.

Measure: retention + energy

Customer Trust

Confidence that Lumify is easier to buy from and more useful.

Measure: renewals + expansion

Vendor Trust

Confidence that Lumify drives adoption, not just authorised delivery.

Measure: co-sell pull
Turnarounds move at the speed of trust.
