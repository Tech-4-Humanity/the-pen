EVIDENCE LEGEND

Claim States

A simple evidence language for the Lumify CEO Command Centre so seeded scenarios are useful but never confused with proven facts.

The five claim states

ACTUAL

Verified from source systems or documents: finance, CRM, board pack, signed actions, contracts or named account evidence.

BRIEFING SAYS

Stated in the shared briefing and accepted as a starting point, pending validation where material.

ASSUMPTION

A working belief required for the strategy to hold. Must be tested before investment or board reliance.

SCENARIO

Seeded future-state or market hypothesis. Useful for discussion, not represented as current Lumify performance.

DATA NEEDED

A known evidence gap. The page can still be useful, but decisions should not be made until this data is supplied.
