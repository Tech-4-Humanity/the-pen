## Core positioning

Lumify is not just a training company with a turnaround problem. It is a distribution network into enterprise capability with a damaged operating model, under-used customer access, and a credible chance to become the adoption layer for technology transformation.

The shared briefing is best read as a disciplined pathway:

- H0.5 — leadership reset and cost discipline
- H1.0 — integration, pricing, utilisation and unified sales
- H2.0 — tested capability and adoption pilots
- H3.0 — sale/refinance readiness and value crystallisation

The possible future layer, if evidence supports it:

- H4.0 — workforce intelligence
- H5.0 — capability infrastructure

## Board-safe framing

This is not a counter-proposal. It is a working interpretation of the shared briefing, intended to test understanding of the sequence, assumptions, risks and possible value-creation pathways.

Confirm → validate → stress-test → extend only when earned.

## Strong meeting line

I am not trying to jump past H1.0. I think H1.0 is what earns the right to everything else. My interest is in making sure that, while we repair the business, we are also building the evidence, customer relationships and operating model that give H2.0 and H3.0 real value.

## Category shift line

H1.0 improves earnings. H2.0 improves growth. H3.0 changes the category. The real value creation comes when Lumify stops being valued only as a training company and starts being valued as the capability and workforce intelligence layer that helps organisations absorb technological change.
