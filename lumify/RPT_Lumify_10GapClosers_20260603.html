The Outcomes That Matter

This is not a job list. It is the board-ready outcome map: what must become clearer, safer, more valuable and more believable before Lumify earns a stronger story.

Purpose

Turn the shared briefing into decision-quality outcomes.

Executive summary

The briefing gives the repair pathway.

The outcome is confidence: cleaner earnings, clearer account upside, proven H2 demand and a credible option to re-rate.

Operating lens

Known → unproven → risk → value.

Every section should help the board make a better decision, not give management another task list.
