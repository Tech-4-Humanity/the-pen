CEO QUESTIONS ATLAS

5 → 12 → 60

A meeting-ready question system for understanding the business you may inherit: five if time is short, twelve domains if the room opens up, sixty drill-downs for due diligence.

The Five

Success

What would make this appointment an outstanding success in 12 months?

Value

What needs to happen for Lumify to be worth 2–3x more?

Customers

Which customers would you personally never want to lose?

Operations

Where do we genuinely make money and where do we leak margin?

Trust

What would make you lose confidence in the incoming CEO?

The Twelve Domains

1 Success

What does winning look like?

2 Value Creation

How does enterprise value grow?

3 Customers

Who pays the bills?

4 Operating Model

What actually makes money?

5 People

Who matters?

6 Board

What keeps the board awake?

7 Owner / PE

What worries a buyer?

8 Financial Truth

What is financially true?

9 H1.0

What must happen first?

10 H2.0

What evidence exists?

11 Role

Why does this role exist now?

12 Trust

What gets a CEO fired?

The Sixty Drill-Downs

Success
  • What would the board celebrate?
  • What would the owner celebrate?
  • What would customers notice?
  • What would employees notice?
  • What metrics matter most?
Value
  • Where does the next $10m come from?
  • Where does the next $25m come from?
  • Where does the next $50m come from?
  • What creates multiple expansion?
  • What destroys it?
Customers
  • Top 20 customers?
  • Top 20 margin?
  • Top 20 growth?
  • Top 20 risk?
  • Why do they leave?
Operations
  • What makes money?
  • What loses money?
  • Which regions win?
  • Which regions struggle?
  • What drives margin?
People
  • Who are the stars?
  • Who are flight risks?
  • Who carries relationships?
  • Who carries knowledge?
  • What skills are missing?
Board
  • Biggest concern?
  • Biggest risk?
  • Biggest frustration?
  • What gets escalated?
  • What proves H1?
Owner / PE
  • Customer concentration?
  • Leadership dependence?
  • Revenue quality?
  • Cash conversion?
  • Exit readiness?
Finance
  • Cash?
  • Debt?
  • Covenants?
  • Audit issues?
  • Forecast confidence?
H1.0
  • What is done?
  • What is blocked?
  • What is political?
  • What creates momentum?
  • What proves success?
H2.0
  • Who has paid?
  • Who has piloted?
  • What is proven?
  • What is assumed?
  • What is repeatable?
Role
  • Why now?
  • Why external?
  • Why me?
  • What does failure look like?
  • What decisions are mine?
Trust
  • What behaviours lose trust?
  • What surprises lose trust?
  • What governance failures lose trust?
  • What leadership failures lose trust?
  • What commercial failures lose trust?
Most candidates answer questions. CEOs decode why the question is being asked.
