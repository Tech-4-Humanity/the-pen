Operating strawman · questions · adjustments

What the Pack Teaches / What Must Be Tied Down

The printed role maps become the source document. Each square can become a working page: what the plan assumes, what evidence is needed, what questions to ask, what may need to change, and whether the board, owners, team and customers actually agree on the plan.

The hard copy is the anchor.

Use the one-page role map as the operating strawman. The website turns each square into evidence, questions, owner logic and adjustment options.

What has been learnt

The mandate is not training-company maintenance.

The real thesis is repair the base, integrate the platform, grow the wallet, prove capability/adoption and create optionality.

Board test

Does everyone agree on the plan?

If not, the first CEO task is not execution. It is alignment around the actual plan, risks, owners, measures and decision gates.
