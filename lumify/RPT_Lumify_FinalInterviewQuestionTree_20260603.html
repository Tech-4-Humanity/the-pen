Final interview · one-page executive question tree

Determine What Is Real

Objective: determine whether H1.0 is real, whether H2.0 is real, how Epsilon wins, and whether this is the right role. Ask once. Then ask for evidence.

CEO rule

After every answer ask: how do you know, what evidence supports that, what have you seen, or can you give me an example?

Do not go backwards

This is not a generic interview. It is a final evidence conversation with Alan and the board.

Listen for numbers

Customers, spend, utilisation, pricing, margin, confidence level, examples, decision thresholds.

Executive question tree

1

What creates the first $1m of EBITDA improvement?

Why: Find the biggest value lever.

Follow: Which customers? How much spend? What stopped capture? Confidence 1-10? Evidence?

Red flag: Generalities, no numbers.

2

What assumption in H1.0 worries you most?

Why: Every turnaround has a weak link.

Follow: What could fail? Tried before? Supporters? Resistors? Plan B?

Red flag: “We're confident in everything.”

3

What is the break-even point for Work AU?

Why: Largest revenue contributor and earnings drag.

Follow: Utilisation, pricing, demand? What changed since FY22? Invest/cut $1m where?

Red flag: Nobody knows the number.

4

What % of top-20 accounts buy multiple brands?

Why: Tests integrated-sales thesis.

Follow: Why not more? Customer example? Current spend? Potential spend? Biggest untapped account?

Red flag: No account-level visibility.

5

Can you show me a customer already behaving as if H2.0 exists?

Why: Separates demand from PowerPoint.

Follow: What did they buy? Budget owner? Why Lumify? Repeat 10x? Repeat 100x?

Red flag: Expert opinion instead of customer proof.

6

What does success look like for Epsilon?

Why: Understand how owner wins.

Follow: EBITDA? Multiple? Refinance? Sale? What happens at $12m or $15m EBITDA?

Red flag: Board and management misalignment.

7

What would cause the board to accelerate a sale?

Why: Reveals endgame.

Follow: Revenue, EBITDA, cash generation, buyer interest, valuation range?

Red flag: No decision criteria.

8

What surprised you most after getting inside the business?

Why: Find hidden truth.

Follow: Better/worse? Biggest misconception? What was not visible externally?

Red flag: Prepared answer.

9

Why did prior leadership remain through the decline?

Why: Tests governance maturity.

Follow: What should have happened sooner? What did the board learn?

Red flag: Blame without ownership.

Closing question

“At the end of 2028, do you want Lumify to be known as the best training company in Australia, or the company that makes transformation stick?”

Their answer revealsWhat it means
Turnaround or growth storyWhether the mandate is repair-only or platform build.
Build or sell storyWhether H3 is sale prep or value expansion.
Training or transformationWhether H2/H4/H5 should exist.
Operator or visionary CEOWhat kind of leader they really want.
H1.0 or H2.0 priorityWhere time, capital and decision rights must go.
Ask for evidence.The second answer is usually more valuable than the first.
