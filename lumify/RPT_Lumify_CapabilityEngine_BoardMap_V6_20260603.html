2 · Change the front door, change the multiple

Current perception risk

  • × Courses, seats, calendars and certifications.
  • × Buyer sits mainly in L&D, HR or IT training.
  • × Revenue is project-like and easier to compare on price.
  • × Value story is EBITDA repair and better utilisation.
  • × Multiple stays close to services/training logic.
Possible re-rate pathway

New front door: capability engine

  • ✓ Capability, adoption, readiness, governance and outcomes.
  • ✓ Buyer expands to CIO, CHRO, CRO, COO and board.
  • ✓ Revenue becomes program, subscription and intelligence-led.
  • ✓ Value story includes data, repeatability and strategic relevance.
  • ✓ Multiple can move toward capability/platform logic if proven.

9 · Data-room checklist: what makes decisions better

Validate H1.0

  • ✓ Top-20 account spend and whitespace.
  • ✓ Pipeline by stage, brand and owner.
  • ✓ Pricing / discount leakage.
  • ✓ Work AU margin bridge and utilisation.
  • ✓ Customer retention, NPS and churn risk.

Validate H2.0

  • ✓ Customers asking beyond training.
  • ✓ Partner co-sell interest.
  • ✓ Pilot economics and delivery effort.
  • ✓ Subscription willingness.
  • ✓ Repeatability and referenceability.

Validate H3/H4

  • ✓ Buyer universe and value drivers.
  • ✓ Recurring revenue proof.
  • ✓ Capability data inventory.
  • ✓ Benchmarking potential by vertical.
  • ✓ Board reporting and data-room readiness.
