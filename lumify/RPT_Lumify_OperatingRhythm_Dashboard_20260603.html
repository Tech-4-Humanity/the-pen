OPERATING RHYTHM

The Business Cadence

How H0.5 becomes H1: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual decision cadence for CEO, CFO, CRO, COO and board.

Five rhythms

Daily

Cash, escalations, customer issues, delivery failures and people heat.

Weekly

Pipeline, renewals, utilisation, cost-out actions and top 20 movement.

Monthly

Actuals, forecast, margin bridge, H1 milestones and board pack.

Quarterly

Strategy, value creation, H2 evidence, people bench and risk reset.

Annual

Budget, asset value, portfolio shape, capital allocation and owner outcome.
