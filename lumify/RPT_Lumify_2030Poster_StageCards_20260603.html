Stage Cards

H0.5 · now · 4–6 weeks

Reset

Briefing says

Leadership reset, cost discipline, ExCo rebuild, board support, immediate stabilisation.

Validate

What cost-out is banked? What is still execution-dependent? Who carries trust?

Unlock

Permission to act; truth baseline; board confidence; reduced drag.

H1.0 · 6–12 months

Integrate

Briefing says

Platform integration, pricing discipline, utilisation, unified sales and margin uplift.

Validate

Top-20 account whitespace, Work AU margin bridge, CRM quality, discount leakage.

Unlock

The asset starts behaving like one company and earns the right to H2.

H2.0 · 12–24 months

Prove Capability / Adoption

Briefing says

Capability/adoption is a hypothesis to test through pilots, not an assumed position.

Validate

Named pilot customers, willingness to pay, economics, channel route and repeatability.

Unlock

Better revenue quality, customer stickiness, subscription tail and category credibility.

H3.0 · 24+ months

Scale / Optionality

Briefing says

Sale/refinance readiness, buyer narrative, governance and value crystallisation.

Validate

Which buyer type values the asset most? EBITDA, customers, vendor access, data or platform?

Unlock

Multiple re-rating and decision flexibility: sell, refinance, hold, acquire or scale.

H4.0 · possible future

Workforce Intelligence

Briefing says

Not named as core, but implied through capability, adoption and subscription tail.

Validate

Data inventory: learners, customers, roles, certifications, adoption, benchmarks.

Unlock

Recurring intelligence, dashboards, readiness scoring and defensible data asset.

H5.0 · possible future

Capability Infrastructure

Briefing says

Not in current sequence; it is the possible extension if H1–H4 are evidenced.

Validate

Industry demand, government/critical infrastructure relevance, partner pull and category right to win.

Unlock

National capability relevance and a different asset category altogether.
