Stage Cards

H0.5 / H1 · 2026

Repair

Briefing says

Leadership reset, cost discipline, ExCo rebuild, board support, stabilisation and integrated operating basics.

Validate

What cost-out is banked? What is dependent? What is Work AU's root cause? Who carries trust?

Unlock

Truth baseline, board confidence, reduced drag and permission to integrate.

H1 · 2027

Integrate

Briefing says

Platform integration, pricing discipline, utilisation, unified sales and margin uplift.

Validate

Top-20 whitespace, CRM quality, discount leakage, sales cadence, account ownership and reporting quality.

Unlock

The asset starts behaving like one company and earns the right to H2.

H2 · 2028

Prove Capability / Adoption

Briefing says

Capability/adoption is a hypothesis to test through pilots, not an assumed position.

Validate

Named pilot customers, willingness to pay, economics, channel route, referenceability and repeatability.

Unlock

Better revenue quality, customer stickiness, subscription tail and category credibility.

H4 option · 2029

Workforce Intelligence

Briefing says

Not day one, but implied if capability, adoption and subscription proof are real.

Validate

Data inventory across learners, roles, customers, certifications, adoption, outcomes and benchmarks.

Unlock

Recurring intelligence, dashboards, readiness scoring and defensible data asset.

H5 · 2030

Capability Infrastructure

Briefing says

Possible extension only if H1–H4 are evidenced.

Validate

Industry demand, government/critical infrastructure relevance, partner pull and category right to win.

Unlock

National capability relevance and a different asset category altogether.

H1 fixes the asset.H2 proves demand.H3/H4 scales repeatability.H5 changes what the asset is.
