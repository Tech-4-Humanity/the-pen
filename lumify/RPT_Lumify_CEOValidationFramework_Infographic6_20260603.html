The CEO Validation Framework

Confirming the facts. Aligning the strategy. Building the plan. This keeps the scrutiny, but changes the posture from interrogation to executive validation.

Core stance

Help me understand what is true, what is assumed, what is proven, and what still needs validation.

Why it works

Aligned, not adversarial.

It respects the briefing while exposing the evidence required to execute.

How to use it

Ask as readiness, not attack.

Every question should make execution safer, clearer and more valuable.

FactWhat it meansEvidence required
