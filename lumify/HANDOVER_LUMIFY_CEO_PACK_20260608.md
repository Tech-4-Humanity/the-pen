## 6. Interview question doctrine

The final interview is not the place to ask for org charts, recruitment plans or full data-room materials.

Those are mutual diligence questions after the final interview or after a verbal offer.

### Final interview questions

Use questions that reveal operating truth without creating friction.

Highest-value questions:

1. What creates the first $1m of EBITDA improvement?
2. What assumption in H1.0 worries you most?
3. What is the break-even point for Work AU?
4. What percentage of top-20 accounts buy multiple brands?
5. Can you show me a customer already behaving as if H2.0 exists?
6. What does success look like for Epsilon?
7. What would cause the board to accelerate a sale?
8. What surprised you most after getting inside the business?
9. Why did prior leadership remain through the decline?

### CEO rule

After every answer, ask one of:

```text
How do you know?
What evidence supports that?
What have you seen that gives you confidence?
Can you give me an example?
```

The second answer is usually more valuable than the first.

### Closing question

```text
At the end of 2028, do you want Lumify to be known as the best training company in Australia, or the company that makes transformation stick?
```

Then stop talking.

## 7. Org chart / recruitment plan doctrine

The user repeatedly wanted the org chart. The final conclusion:

Do not ask directly in the final interview.

Ask this bridge question instead:

```text
As you think about H1 and H2, what capabilities are you adding to the organisation today that were not needed three years ago?
```

This reveals:

- hiring priorities
- operating model direction
- organisation design
- H2 seriousness
- future capability intent

Save direct org chart, vacancies and recruitment plan questions for mutual diligence.

## 8. Email doctrine

Pre-meeting email should not ask for homework.

Purpose:

- show the briefing has been read
- show the sequencing is understood
- signal CEO-level thinking
- avoid appearing hard work
- avoid creating a data request before the meeting

Best email line:

```text
I am also interested in understanding where Lumify already has permission to play through its customers, partners, brand and existing strengths.
```

Do not include:

- top-20 data request
- management pack request
- org chart request
- debt/covenant request
- detailed diligence list

Those belong in the room or after the final interview.
