Investor-safe adjacency map

Everything on the right comes from assets already on the left.

This is the non-threatening way to show future optionality. Do not start with a future-state diagram. Start with the business Lumify already has: sales, marketing, partnerships, delivery, products, operations and customers.

Board-safe message

This is not becoming a different company. It is exposing adjacent opportunities created by existing customers, partners, courses, delivery capability and sales motion.

Why it matters

A lender or PE owner does not want a futurist leap. They want evidence that existing assets can create attractive adjacent economics.

CEO bridge question

As you think about H1 and H2, what capabilities are being added to the organisation today that were not needed three years ago?
