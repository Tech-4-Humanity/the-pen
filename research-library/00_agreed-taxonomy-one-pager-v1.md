## Executive correction

The previous version made two structural mistakes:

1. It treated Emerging as a theme. That was wrong. Emerging is a decision status / parking lot.
2. It let Social Media disappear. That was wrong. Social Media is a real body of work and operating surface.

The correct model is:

Programme / Body of Work -> Topic Family -> Topic -> Subtopic / Asset -> Decision Status

Decision statuses are:

- KEEP
- MERGE
- PARK
- KILL
- EMERGING
- DUPLICATE
- PROMOTE

Emerging, parked, killed and merged are not themes. They are states applied to rows.
